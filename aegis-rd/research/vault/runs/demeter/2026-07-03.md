### 20260703T171349334411Z_demeter_eu_credit_carry_income vs 20260703T171429427798Z_demeter_eu_credit_carry_sharpe - the ranker A/B

- **Hypothesis**: "The ranker A/B" from [[what-makes-a-carry-sleeve-an-income-engine]] -
  Sharpe-ranking a short-gamma sweep selects the cell that best hides its tail, so on an
  identical 54-cell grid (carry_window {126,252,504} x vol_window {21,63} x vol_target
  {0.06,0.10,0.15} x carry_gain {0,1,2}, same seed and splits) the MPPM-ranked champion
  (`carry_income.yaml`) should differ from the sharpe-ranked champion
  (`carry_sharpe.yaml`, control arm) and dominate it on the tail axis at comparable
  income.
- **Keep/kill criteria** (pre-registered, before launching):
  - KEEP if, on the Held-out set, the income-arm champion vs the sharpe-arm champion:
    `carry_income_utility` >= AND `carry_tail_budget` (2-6mo left-decile mean) strictly
    shallower AND held-out Sharpe within 0.10.
  - NULL (no win claimed) if both rankers select the same grid cell - record as the
    Eling-Schuhmacher elliptical null on this book; keep the metric as a free trust
    check, do not iterate on it.
  - KILL the ranker hypothesis if the MPPM champion is deeper-tailed OR gives up more
    than 0.10 held-out Sharpe with no tail improvement.
  - Trust checks before any keep: rho-stability of the ranking across rho in [2,5]
    (`carry_utility_rho_sensitivity_from_curve`); champion not on a grid edge.
- **What changed since previous run**: params unpinned (54-cell sweep, both arms);
  control arm added so the comparison is champion-vs-champion under the engine's own
  selection, not a post-hoc re-rank.
- **Result**: the champions DIFFER. Sharpe arm re-selects the existing lock's cell
  (cw252 / vw63 / vt0.10 / cg2.0 - the control reproduces the champion, clean test);
  the MPPM arm picks a lower-scale, faster-vol cell (cw252 / vw21 / **vt0.06** / cg2.0).
  Held-out champion-vs-champion (cross-metrics from pin-runs
  `20260703T171148105248Z` (MPPM on the sharpe cell) and the two
  `credit_carry_diag` tail-budget runs `20260703T171636282032Z` / `20260703T171658407815Z`):

  | held-out (6-split mean) | MPPM champ | Sharpe champ | pre-registered bar | outcome |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | carry_income_utility | +0.0081 | **+0.0100** | MPPM champ >= | **FAIL** |
  | carry_tail_budget | **-0.0985** | -0.1241 | strictly shallower | PASS |
  | sharpe_ratio | 0.376 | 0.462 | within 0.10 (gap 0.085) | PASS |
  | max_dd (crisis split 1) | **14.2%** | 21.7% | (context) | - |

  The mechanism worked as designed - the MPPM champion is genuinely shallower-tailed
  (tail budget -9.9% vs -12.4%/yr, crisis-split maxDD 14.2% vs 21.7%, crisis return
  -7.4% vs -8.8%) at the cost of calm income (return 1.31 vs 2.00). But it failed its
  own held-out bar: at rho=3 the certainty-equivalent says the sharpe cell's extra calm
  income is worth its deeper tail (0.0100 > 0.0081), i.e. the cell MPPM prefers on the
  selection windows is not the cell utility prefers out-of-sample.
- **Verdict**: **iterate**. Not KEEP (utility bar failed), not NULL (cells differ), not
  KILL (the MPPM champion is shallower-tailed and inside the 0.10 sharpe band).
  Diagnosis: on this long-only, gross-capped (<=1), vol-targeted book the Sharpe-gaming
  attack surface is structurally small - the grid has no leverage or optionality axis to
  hide tail in, and its dominant free axis is *scale* (vol_target). Sharpe is
  scale-invariant and never spends its ranking on that axis; MPPM prices scale
  (CE-optimal s* ~ mu/(rho sigma^2)) and therefore spends its ranking on the axis where
  mu-estimation noise lives - the selection/held-out flip is scale-estimation noise,
  not tail-hiding detection. Iterate directions: (a) a shape-only variant - MPPM on the
  vol-standardized stream - so the ranker orders payoff shapes and the mandate sets
  scale; (b) the Eling-Schuhmacher-adjacent fallback: keep sharpe as ranker, adopt
  carry_income_utility + carry_tail_budget as promotion gates (both are now cheap,
  registered metrics); the tail-budget gap measured here (-9.9 vs -12.4%/yr on the same
  book) shows the gate has real discriminating power even when the ranker A/B is a wash.

## Day summary

The carry pole got its bespoke selection machinery and its first honest test. Built from
[[what-makes-a-carry-sleeve-an-income-engine]]: `carry_income_utility` (MPPM, rho=3,
manipulation-proof) and `carry_tail_budget` (2-6mo left-decile band average), both
registered opt-in; `carry_income.yaml` / `carry_sharpe.yaml` as a two-arm ranker A/B on
the duration-stripped credit book. The A/B came back *iterate*: the MPPM arm selects a
genuinely shallower-tailed champion (its whole point) but loses its own held-out
utility bar to the sharpe cell, because on a gross-capped long-only book the metric's
ranking budget is spent on the noisy scale axis rather than on tail-hiding (there is
none available to hide). The metrics survive as gates; the ranker swap is not promoted.
Also fixed on the way: `demeter_eu.credit_carry` now accepts Nautilus instrument-id
columns (str(col) match; venue-qualified ids added to `_CREDIT_LONG`).

Then the pole got its research-backed construction pass. New article
[[the-ucits-constrained-carry-sleeve]] sorted the levers: adopt the defensive maturity
tilt and the EM-local FX-skew leg as tests; reject fallen angels (unstrippable duration)
and trend gates (they flip the pole's skew convex) a priori. The `carry_sota.yaml`
sweep - shape-only grid, vol target pinned by mandate, `carry_income_utility` as the
now-legitimate ranker - came back **keep**: the full short-duration tilt (SDHY for
IHYU) beats the incumbent on utility at a shallower tail with no income give-up and no
concavity loss, while the EM local leg died at every weight on this window. Net: one
article-driven upgrade confirmed, one killed, and the day's two threads closed into
each other - the metric built in the morning ranked the strategy built in the evening.

The evening session (floor re-verification + deep research + the AT1 test) then
overturned two foundations. First, the floor check showed the standalone-optimized
champion had traded away its portfolio role (qskew -1.83 -> -0.49, corr-to-trend
flipped positive) - the research answer is [[the-skew-is-the-product]] and its
composite-gate/DTS-pin/cadence hypotheses. Second, the AT1 tradability-and-income test
exposed that **every backtest to date ran distributing ETFs price-only** (the
distribution store had no production writer; bd issue filed): the carry book was
missing ~4-6%/yr of income per leg, and with coupons seeded the champion cell's
held-out return is +3.58%/yr, not +0.57%/yr - "why is the income engine so poor" was
answered by the plumbing, not the premium. The AT1 leg itself died its pre-registered
death fairly (the vol-target sizes away a high-vol leg regardless of its coupon), and
two pipeline defects (calendar-union NaNs, the distribution store) got fixed or filed
with regression coverage. Every income-based selection made before the seeding -
including the live carry_sota lock - is now provisional pending the re-run cascade in
Next.
