# 2026-07-11

## Goal

First in-pipeline test of the commodity-carry breadth leg (aegis-rd-bkom, GH #80 carry Q1),
now that aegis-rd-tggo delivers quote-marked data for the thin UEQC line. Does adding a
market-neutral commodity-carry ETF (UBS CMCI Commodity Carry SF UCITS, UEQC.IBIS:QUOTE) as a
fixed breadth share lift the concave income pole's Sharpe while its robust downside L-skew
stays < 0 - the leg mutes but must not breach the concave gate? The banked cheap-public-data
signal (bkom) put the lift at income Sharpe 0.87 -> 1.08; this confirms it in-pipeline with
the real quote-driven mark and touch fill.

## Day summary

Three outcomes, one large. (1) The commodity-carry breadth leg (aegis-rd-bkom) ran in-pipeline
for the first time on quote-marked UEQC data: the whole aegis-rd-tggo path fired end to end -
UEQC.IBIS:QUOTE declared like any instrument, cold-fetched BID + ASK from the gateway back to
its 2020-01-23 inception, derived the mid mark, filled at the touch, no fill-cost parameter.
The keep/kill itself is underpowered (UEQC's late inception gives only 3 folds); the 0.25 cell
looks promising and sign-agrees with the banked estimate, but the folds can't tell the weights
apart, so the verdict is iterate, not keep. (2) The run surfaced a REAL bug in the freshly
shipped tggo quote path: the gap-fill serves overlapping yearly batches, so a DENSE series
(BID/ASK) persists two bars for one ts_event at each batch boundary (UEQC: 2020-01-23,
2022-12-30). Nautilus' identity dedupe keeps both (values/ts_init differ), and the catalog read
seam did not collapse per-ts_event, so the quote-mid frame carried a duplicated index and the
strategy's reindex failed. Sparse LAST never hit it - UEQC is the first dense quote series to
run. Fixed at the single read seam (CatalogBackedDataPort._query_bars now collapses to one bar
per ts_event, last wins), which self-heals the already-persisted dups with no re-fetch. (3) The
E50PW put-write challenger then completed after the extension/no-data fix. Its 30% cell is
strong on point estimates, but the response is non-monotone, one fold loses the concave sign,
and four cells over three folds are indistinguishable (p~=0.08). Verdict: iterate, no promotion.
