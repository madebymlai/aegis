# 2026-07-06

## Goal

First margin-priced leverage experiment (aegis-rd-0osb, GH #80 campaign): does removing
demeter's gross <= 1.0 exposure clamp (the top half of its own vol-targeting signal,
computed every bar and discarded) beat the clamped champion once the borrow is charged
at the IBKR-IE EUR blend? Enabled by the margin-interest epic (aegis-rd-5wts): leverage
is no longer free in the research sim, so the unclip test is honest for the first time.

  funds, not account-level margin; the clamp is a design choice, now an evidenced one).

## Day summary

The margin-interest machinery (aegis-rd-5wts) did its job on first use: before it, this
exact candidate would have shown ~+3-4pp/yr of phantom alpha from free leverage and
plausibly displaced the champion; priced, it is a utility wash with worse survivability
gates. Two durable lessons: (1) sleeve-level leverage on a low-net-carry income pole
cannot pay its own funding - the diversification dividend is only worth re-levering at
the book level, where the borrow finances the BLEND's Sharpe (B1), not one sleeve's;
(2) the excess-basis Sharpe tie confirms the sim's funding arithmetic end-to-end
(charged full rate into raw returns, spread-only into excess - both landed where theory
puts them).
