## Goal

Build and verify the floor's **concave pole** (family #2, codename demeter): a carry /
mean-reversion short-gamma income sleeve, the counterparty to atalanta's long-gamma
trend pole. The construction is the structural mirror of the atalanta straddle - the
same vol-scaled conviction-weighted long/short machinery fed a CONTRARIAN score (fade
short-horizon moves = liquidity provision / mean reversion) instead of a trend score.
Today: confirm the realized stream is genuinely **short gamma** to its own universe
(the mirror of atalanta's own-gamma test) and passes the Carry/Mean-Reversion family
gate, on the same 16-asset non-equity book atalanta locked, before formalizing through
the pipeline. Theory anchors:
[[carry-as-the-short-gamma-income-pole]] (the negative-skew signature lives in
FX/commodities) and [[short-horizon-reversal-in-small-cross-sections]] (reversal is
liquidity provision, predictable by VIX, short gamma by construction).

## Day summary

straddle's score to a contrarian one on the same machinery and same book produces a
sleeve that is genuinely short-gamma to its own trend efficiency (own-gamma confirmed,

reversal sits below the transaction-cost line. This validates the roster's claim that
the concave sleeve is the hardest piece to source - and narrows the search: the floor's
income half has to be real **carry** (a yield/roll signal), not a price-reversion
proxy. The same-day substrate test (a carry-tilted 10-asset book: TLT GLD UUP PDBC USL
DBB DBA FXY TIP BWX) sharpened the point: a better universe *cleaned up the concave
shape* (negative skew now appears even in the absolute straddle, eff-slope t to -5.8)
but left the drift negative (Sharpe -0.95 to -2.4), so the blocker is the **signal, not
the substrate**. Widening to a 20-asset book added the breadth axis to the test: the
loss *magnitude* shrank (the formal pipeline's best held-out Sharpe rose from -1.065 on
the 10-asset book to -0.352 on the 20-asset book) but never crossed zero, so breadth is
not the missing piece either. The blocker was the **signal**: price reversion *pays* the
premium, it does not collect it.

The pivot to true carry resolved it the same session. Distribution-yield carry (trailing
dividends / price - bond coupon, credit/EM spread, FX interest, annualized) is concave
**and** positive-drift, the income engine the floor needs. Formalized through the
pipeline (the `Dividends` array loads via the component's declared inputs; the
total-return adjusted Close means the book collects the carry), it locked at positive
held-out Sharpe ~0.79 with 5/5 positive splits - **family #2's concave pole is reached**.
The negative skew is mild (the carry we can measure from distributions is
bond/credit/EM/FX-heavy, not the strongly-concave commodity-roll kind), and the strong
2018-2024 Sharpe wants out-of-time scrutiny, but the qualitative result is decisive:
**carry collects, reversion pays** - and the floor's hardest piece, the concave income
sleeve, now exists.

The "sharpen the concavity" next-step then resolved the mild-skew caveat, web-research
first. The single strongest source is the **variance risk premium** (short vol), and the
reason is structural, not incremental: trend is mechanically *long* variance, so *short*
variance is its exact mirror on the convexity axis. The `demeter.vol_carry` gate (VIX
term-structure signal, short-VXX execution) passed every concave-pole axis and turned the
two weak ones decisive - quarterly skew -1.18 (vs -0.13) and crisis-conditional -0.95% (vs
-0.16% at matched vol) - while keeping positive drift (Sharpe +0.57). So the champion
concave pole is now the VRP sleeve; distribution carry is demoted to a milder secondary
income source. The floor is no longer "trend paired with a thin unrelated carry" but a
**long-variance / short-variance barbell**, the cleanest possible net-skew pairing.
