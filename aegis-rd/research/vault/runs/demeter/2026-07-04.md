# 2026-07-04

## Goal

Build and validate the *allocator metric* the floor re-verification demanded: a
ranker that sees the carry pole's portfolio job, not just its standalone stream. Deep
exa search on the metric shape, then implement + validate. Article layer:
[[what-makes-a-carry-sleeve-an-income-engine]] gains a "The metric to rank on" section.

## Runs

### allocator-metric validation (diagnostic, not a sweep)

Not an aerd run - a metric build with a real-data validation. Harness
`scratchpad/allocator_validate.py` reuses `floor_blend_verify.build_pole_portfolio` to
reconstruct the live champion carry pole and the trend pole through the real pipeline
(distributions now seeded), aligns them (1767 obs, 2018-01..2024-12), and computes the
new metrics.

- **What was built** (all in `metrics/custom/carry.py`, 590 unit tests green):
  - `carry_downside_lskew` (registered opt-in) - robust L-moment skew (tau_3) on
    overlapping multi-month windows, replacing the outlier-dominated quarterly moment
    skew as the concave family-membership gate (Kim-White, Hosking, Bastianin).
  - `composite_allocator_utility` (delta_theta) + `composite_book_utility` +
    `downside_correlation` - the composite ranker: the pole's manipulation-proof
    certainty-equivalent contribution to the blended book (trend + candidate at w=0.40,
    each leg pinned to book vol so it ranks placement not scale), plus the
    Ang-Chen-Xing downside-correlation guard.
- **Design validation** (unit tests): two carry candidates with an *identical* return
  multiset (identical standalone MPPM/tail/skew) are correctly separated by
  delta_theta - the co-crashing permutation scores lower, 20/20 seeds - the property no
  single-book metric can have. And the leg-vol-pinning fix: an earlier design that
  renormalized the *blend* to fixed vol perversely rewarded co-crashing (concentration
  raises raw book vol, and dividing by it shrinks the joint tail); pinning each *leg*
  and letting the blend vol float fixes it.
- **Real-data readout** (live champion vs trend):

  | quantity | value | reading |
  |---|---|---|
  | carry standalone MPPM | +0.0344 | pays income - looks fine |
  | robust L-skew (new gate) | -0.098 | borderline concave (agrees with 2y qskew -0.49) |
  | OLD quarterly moment skew | -0.59 (28 q) | overstates depth ~6x - the estimator noise |
  | full-sample corr to trend | +0.157 | mild |
  | downside corr to trend | +0.422 | co-crashes - the guard fires |
  | **delta_theta (allocator)** | **-0.0094** | **pole SUBTRACTS book CE** |

- **Verdict**: **keep** (the metric). It reproduces the floor verdict in one number -
  the current pole subtracts certainty-equivalent from the book despite a healthy
  standalone MPPM - and the robust skew agrees with the two-year-split qskew where the
  moment skew did not. The metric is the tool the re-run cascade needs.

### the re-run cascade - composite re-rank of the 27-cell grid (diagnostic)

Harness `scratchpad/cascade_rerun.py`: every cell (defensive x fx_weight x carry_gain,
vol_target 0.10, at1 0) rebuilt through the real pipeline (dividends seeded), aligned to
the trend pole, scored on standalone income (MPPM) + the allocator triad (delta_theta,
downside correlation to trend, full-window robust L-skew). Ranked by delta_theta.

- **Pre-registered gate** ([[the-skew-is-the-product]]): promote only a cell with
  delta_theta > 0 (adds book CE), L-skew clearly < 0 (concave), and downside corr to
  trend <= 0 (diversifying in the states the floor is bought).
- **Result**: the gate promotes **nothing**. All 27 cells:
  - `delta_theta` in [-0.0189, -0.0050] - **every cell subtracts book CE** (Theta_trend
    ~+0.094; best book ~+0.089).
  - `downside corr to trend` in **[+0.38, +0.49]** - **no cell diversifies**; the whole
    UCITS credit family co-crashes with the trend book in trend's worst decile.
  - L-skew in [-0.22, -0.08] - genuinely (if shallowly) concave, but concavity is
    useless when it lands *with* trend's losses, not against them.
  - Cell ordering: delta_theta tracks income (since the correlation term is ~uniformly
    bad, it does not differentiate) - the top cells are the highest-income, LOWEST
    carry_gain ones. The composite-best is **def1/fx0.15/cg0** (-0.0050) and
    **def1/fx0/cg0** (-0.0053); the standalone-ranked old champion **def1/fx0.15/cg2**
    ranks 15th (-0.0094). The spread-richness lean (cg2) that won the standalone tail
    story is composite-*negative*: it adds self-vol and tail without helping the book.
- **Verdict**: **kill the floor-partner promotion; pivot the pole to standalone income.**
  Two distinct conclusions: (1) *the floor pairing is dead on this window/universe* - not
  one cell can be a floor partner, because credit and the trend book's risk legs crash
  together (Koijen recession-clustering; credit = short equity-vol), which is the
  structural, regime-conditional limit [[the-skew-is-the-product]] predicted; the metric
  now *proves* it across the whole grid rather than inferring it from one champion. (2)
  *even as pure income, the old champion is dominated* - drop carry_gain to 0; the
  income-best gate-respecting cell is def1/fx0/cg0 (MPPM +0.0479, L-skew -0.16) or the
  composite-best def1/fx0.15/cg0.

### VRP re-source probe (cheap directional diagnostic)

Before committing to a VRP re-source (the sourcing article's crowned concave source, and
the natural read after credit failed the cascade), a cheap composite check against the
current trend champion. Harness `scratchpad/vrp_probe.py` (yfinance proxies, USD vs
EUR-book so treat the *sign* as indicative): covered-call QYLD (equity-VRP) and SVXY
(pure short-vol), each aligned to the trend pole, same allocator triad as the cascade.

- **covered-call (QYLD)**: downside corr **+0.32**, delta_theta -0.017 - still co-crashes
  (its equity crash factor overlaps the trend book's everything-down days), and worse for
  the book than most credit cells (the equity beta the instrument addendum warned about).
- **pure short-vol (SVXY)**: downside corr **+0.28** (the *least* co-crashing of any
  source - consistent with VRP being trend's truest mirror), but income -2.6 and
  delta_theta -0.039: the unhedged Volmageddon tail ruins it, and no survivable UCITS
  form exists (no put-write UCITS; covered-call dilutes with co-crashing equity beta).
- **Verdict**: the VRP re-source does **not** rescue the floor on this window. Every
  investable concave source - credit (+0.38..+0.49), equity-VRP (+0.32), pure vol (+0.28)
  - has *positive* downside correlation to this trend book. The trend champion's worst
  decile is broad risk-off/everything-down (2022-style), where all short-gamma income
  loses together (Koijen recession-clustering, realized against this specific book). The
  gradient (vol < equity-VRP < credit) confirms the sourcing theory - the purer the
  variance short, the less it co-crashes - but even the purest does not cross zero, and
  its survivable form is unavailable.

## Day summary

The allocator question is answered and tooled. Rank on the *composite*: the pole's
manipulation-proof CE contribution to the book (delta_theta), gated on a robust
L-moment skew (family membership), a tail budget (survivability), and a
downside-correlation ceiling to trend (the crisis-conditional guard). Skew is a mandate
the pole must hold, never an objective to maximize. All built, unit-validated
(role-fidelity: identical-standalone candidates separated by placement), and confirmed
on real data: the live champion scores delta_theta -0.0094 (subtracts book CE), which
is the floor verdict as a scalar. The metric replaces the noisy quarterly moment skew
with an outlier-resistant estimator that finally agrees with the rest of the evidence.

Then the metric was pointed at the whole grid (the re-run cascade) and past the credit
universe (the VRP probe), and it returned an uncomfortable, decisive answer: **on this
window, against this trend champion, no investable concave source is a floor partner.**
All 27 credit cells co-crash with trend (downside corr +0.38..+0.49); covered-call VRP
co-crashes (+0.32); even pure short-vol - the least-co-crashing at +0.28, the sourcing
theory vindicated - does not cross zero and has no survivable UCITS form. The trend
champion's worst decile is broad everything-down, where every short-gamma income stream
loses together. This does not contradict [[the-skew-is-the-product]] - it confirms its
own conclusion: the floor is *regime-conditional*, and the composite metric now proves
it across every source rather than inferring it from one champion. The construction
levers (DTS-pin, cadence, DBXM's undilutable spread) all deepen the pole's skew - but a
deeper-skew pole co-crashes *harder* in the shared everything-down state, so they fix
family membership without fixing the binding relational constraint. The next experiment
is not another instrument; it is the trend-winter stress read - measure the floor in the
regime it was designed for, with the metric now in hand.

### config wiring + dividend-aware lock replay

- **`report.metrics` config field added** (schema + resolution + cross-check + tests):
  extra opt-in custom metrics computed and reported per candidate beside the ranker
  (previously only `ranking.metric` pulled a custom metric). `carry_sota.yaml` and
  `carry_sota_at1.yaml` now report `[carry_tail_budget, carry_downside_lskew]`.
- **Lock forward-compat: considered, then rejected.** The v1.1.0 `at1_weight` bump
  broke *replay* of the pre-at1 lock (a param that did not exist when the lock was
  minted). A first fix silently defaulted absent locked params from the manifest;
  reverted on review - it is a backward-compat shim on a reproducibility contract
  (against Forward-First) and, worse, *silent*: safe only because at1_weight's default
  is a no-op, but a future active-default param would silently change what old locks
  replay to. The forward-first fix is to **re-lock** (the re-run cascade does this); if
  lock migration is ever wanted it must be loud (fail naming the param+version, or
  default-and-record-in-provenance), never a silent setdefault. Consequence: `aerd run`
  on `carry_sota.yaml` (a lock replay) fails loud until re-locked - the honest signal
  that the lock is stale.
- **Dividends now flow end-to-end** (distributions seeded, GH #76): the pipeline loads
  269 events into the carry book and reports coverage. The champion book with coupons
  compounds to ~**+4.76%/yr** total return (vs +1.61% price-only) with full-window
  `carry_income_utility` ~+0.034 - the income the plumbing had been hiding. Robust skew
  read full-window (`allocator_validate.py`): `carry_downside_lskew` **-0.098**, i.e.
  only *borderline* concave (agrees with the 2y-split qskew -0.49) - the champion is an
  income stream, not the deep short-gamma anchor the floor hired. NB the split-mean of
  this gate on the standard 504/0.5 splits is noisy (few multi-month windows per ~1y
  split, same caveat as qskew) - read the family gate on long windows (full-window or
  1008/max_splits 3), not the per-split mean.
