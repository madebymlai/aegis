# 2026-07-25 - demeter

## Context

The convergent pole was audited for instrument correctness rather than for signal. Two of
the four instruments in the incumbent config carried zero weight in every row
(`defensive: 1.0` zeroes IHYU's tilt, `fx_weight: 0.0` zeroes IEML), so the live sleeve
was SDHY + LQDH. Both are USD-denominated lines held in a EUR book.

Measured on total return rebuilt from the catalog distribution store, 2018-2026:

| leg | vol in USD | vol in EUR | FX share of variance | corr(asset, USD) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SDHY | 6.64% | 8.73% | 67.7% | -0.20 |
| LQDH | 7.06% | 9.49% | 57.3% | -0.11 |

EURUSD runs 7.18%/yr, as volatile as the credit assets themselves. About two thirds of the
pole's risk was an unhedged currency position with no expected return. The realized EUR
return exceeded the USD return over this sample, but that is a realized FX gain, not a
premium.

The second finding matters more for the pole's role: **SDHY and LQDH correlate +0.753 in
EUR terms**. The two-leg construction was substantially one bet, and the shared dollar is
part of why. Every EUR-native pairing measured better (XHYG+SYBD +0.31, XHYG+IRCP +0.56,
IHYG+IRCP +0.64).

Instrument verification against the live IB gateway found two traps worth recording:
`SEUC` resolves to the CHF line on SIX (taking it would have re-imported the currency
problem the swap exists to remove) and the EUR line of that fund is `SYBD` on Xetra;
`EUNW` and `ECR3` have no IBKR security definition at all. Separately, the €1,118m
"iShares Euro Corporate Bond Interest Rate Hedged" that dominates search results
(`DE000A1J7CL2`) has been liquidated or merged - the live rate-hedged EUR IG fund is
`IRCP` / `IE00B6X2VY59` at €213m.

### PENDING - demeter_eu_credit_pair (unlocked, store-minting)

**Hypothesis.** Removing the currency from the convergent pole raises its certainty-
equivalent contribution to the trend-paired book, because roughly two thirds of the risk
it was contributing carried no compensation and part of its measured co-movement with
trend was shared dollar length rather than shared credit.

**Universe.** STHE (PIMCO US HY Constrained 0-5yr, EUR-hedged share class of the fund
[[the-ucits-constrained-carry-sleeve]] already names as the co-equal expression of
"defensive inside HY" - a share-class change inside the article's own shortlist, not a new
instrument hypothesis), IHYG (EUR-native HY, a second credit market), IRCP (EUR IG with
rates stripped via German government bond futures, the EUR analogue of LQDH; the article's
instrument survey recorded the rate-hedged wrapper as existing "for IG (LQDH)" and did not
have a EUR one).

**Grid.** shape_power {0.0, 1.0} x eur_hy_share {0.0, 0.5, 1.0}, 6 cells. `shape_power`
exists because inverse-vol weighting - which carry_mix hard-coded - hands the low-vol IG
leg 66-73% of the sleeve, so the IG leg silently becomes the sleeve. Nested control at
(1.0, 0.0) is STHE+IRCP, the straight currency-fixed analogue of SDHY+LQDH.

**Deliberately NOT changed.** `exposure = min(vol_target / sigma_book, 1.0)` is carried
over verbatim despite binding on 81-94% of days and despite the book allocator already
vol-targeting and levering to gross_cap 1.75. The article's own cited evidence has
volatility-managing HY buying Sharpe by reducing the negative skew, which for a pole hired
to be concave is paying out of the skew inventory - a real experiment, but bundling it
into a universe swap would confound the two changes.

**Pre-registered keep/kill.** Judged on the Held-out split:

- **KEEP** if the currency-fixed control cell (shape_power 1.0, eur_hy_share 0.0) shows
  `delta_theta` above the incumbent's live reading, AND the downside correlation to trend
  falls below the incumbent's **+0.2724**, AND `convergent_tail_budget` is no worse than the
  incumbent's.
