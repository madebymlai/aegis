# 2026-07-05

## Goal

No new runs - tooling. Port the P1/P2 floor-gate harness out of the (session-mortal)
scratchpad into a durable on-demand script, so the gate/ranking read survives the
scratchpad's deletion and can be re-run when a carry cell or pole changes.

## Work

`scripts/floor_gate.py` (new): builds both poles' daily streams through the real
component pipeline (the `floor_blend_verify.build_pole_portfolio` method) and reports:

- the recalibrated gate at the floor's MONTHLY horizon (delta_theta with pp=12,
  leg vol book_vol/sqrt(12), no blend renormalize; |corr| < 0.10),
- the `--exclude-month` fast-crash decomposition (P1),
- the floor arbiter block: blend-vs-trend-alone Sharpe/maxDD (P2 recalibration),
- daily delta_theta as reference only.

Carry cell comes from the config's pinned `strategy.params` (never a hardcoded cell -
the old scratchpad's `CARRY_CHAMPION_PARAMS` was the stale fx0.15/cg2 cell), with
`vol_target=0.10` always applied (offline path reads manifest defaults, not the
collapsed param_space). `--carry-param k=v` scores alternative cells on demand - this
is the delta_theta ranking tripwire from [[runs/aegis/2026-07-05|2026-07-05 (aegis)]]: if delta_theta
ordering ever disagrees with the income ranker's ordering on stored roles, that is the
trigger to build the sweep-time fixture (deferred in [[2026-07-04]], deliberately -
within-family delta_theta tracks income, so the sweep ranker stays income).

**Reproduction check** (champion def1/fx0/cg0, 2018-2024, 84 months): monthly
delta_theta -0.0115, monthly corr +0.0497, ex-COVID (+3 months dropped) +0.0118 -
matches the P2 record (-0.0115 / +0.050 / +0.0117) to rounding.
