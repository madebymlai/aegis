# 2026-06-12

## Goal

Atalanta is the floor's **trend / momentum long-gamma pole** (family #1), and its
vol-scaled long/short straddle reads flat on quadratic convexity ($\beta_2 \approx 0$
vs SPY) across every universe and window tested. An external literature review
(`~/Downloads/Atalanta Convexity Research Handoff.pdf`, distilled in bd memory
`atalanta-convexity-research-distilled`) proposed six rescue hypotheses. Today tests
the two that are both *new to our vault* and *trend-preserving* (not a pivot to a
defensive sleeve): is the flat $\beta_2$ a daily-**measurement** artifact (H1), or is
our per-bar sizing **capping** the convex term (H2)? The other four are already
settled for us: the barbell (H4) is in the graveyard (fast leg added whipsaw in our
thin book, 2026-06-10 v6); turning-point speed-blending (H5) is covered in
[[trend-following-in-whipsaw-regimes]]; the futures universe (H3) and VWAP/intraday
execution (H6) need infrastructure we do not have.

These are gate-metric **diagnostics** (harness recomputes the convexity signatures on
the frozen winner stream), not optimizer runs, so they have no `runs/` run_id.
Winner under test: `atalanta.trendStraddle` v2 lb252 / entry_band 0.05 / vol_window 21,
10-ETF book, real yf 2019-2024 (warmup trimmed). Theory anchor: the CFM identity
(`[[convexity-as-the-axis-of-strategy-diversification]]`, [^cfmconv]) - trend P&L
$= \tfrac12[(\Sigma\delta p)^2 - \Sigma(\delta p)^2]$, so the gamma lives in the
long-horizon squared-move term.

## Runs

### H1 - convexity measurement horizon (diagnostic)

- **Hypothesis**: the gate's $\beta_2$ is a *daily* Treynor-Mazuy fit; the convex term
  is multi-period, so $\beta_2$ should turn positive at monthly/quarterly horizon where
  daily reads ~0. Harness `/tmp/atalanta_h1_horizon.py`.
- **Keep/kill criteria** (pre-registered): CONFIRM (gate mismeasures) if monthly
  $\beta_2 > 0$ for the winner and materially above its daily value, ideally
  significant (p < 0.10). KILL (non-convexity is real) if monthly AND quarterly
  $\beta_2 \le 0$.
- **What changed**: re-measure $\beta_2$ at daily / weekly / monthly / quarterly with
  an OLS t-stat; both the 10-ETF and curated-14 winners.
- **Result**: $\beta_2$ does NOT turn positive at longer horizons - it drifts slightly
  *more negative* and stays insignificant. 10-ETF: daily +0.0000 (p .90), weekly
  -0.0003, monthly -0.0028 (t -1.18), quarterly -0.0071. cur14: daily -0.0000, monthly
  -0.0028, quarterly -0.0062. Measuring at the payoff horizon makes it look mildly
  *concave*, not convex.
- **Verdict**: **kill**. The flat $\beta_2$ is not a daily-measurement artifact; the
  convexity is genuinely absent at every horizon. (The methodological point - fit
  $\beta_2$ at the payoff horizon - still stands for a genuinely convex stream; ours
  is not one.)

### H2 - does per-bar sizing cap the convex term? (diagnostic)

- **Hypothesis**: renormalizing $\Sigma|w|=1.0$ every bar prevents the book from
  growing as a trend persists, capping the $(\Sigma\delta p)^2$ macro term that IS the
  gamma. Let gross float by conviction (and remove the inverse-vol throttle) and
  $\beta_2$ should turn positive. Harnesses `/tmp/atalanta_h2_sizing.py`,
  `/tmp/atalanta_h2b_volthrottle.py`.
- **Keep/kill criteria** (pre-registered): CONFIRM if a floating-gross variant lifts
  monthly $\beta_2$ above 0 while the book demonstrably expands on SPY worst-decile
  days. KILL if $\beta_2$ stays $\le 0$ across floating-gross and vol-throttle
  variants.
- **What changed**: (a) floating gross = scale $w \propto$ score/vol by a constant so
  the book floats with conviction, clip at cap; (b) slow vol (vol_window 252) and
  sign-only sizing to stop the inverse-vol term de-risking into the crash.
- **Result**: $\beta_2$ stays negative everywhere. Baseline (renorm) monthly $\beta_2$
  -0.0028; floating score/vol21 -0.0010 (book *contracts* in stress, gCrash 0.49 <
  0.55 - the /vol term throttles down as crash vol spikes); float score/vol252 -0.0011
  (book now *expands*, gCrash 0.51 > 0.50, but $\beta_2$ still negative); float
  sign-only -0.0016 with qskew collapsing to -1.23 (the v1 whipsaw). Floating gross
  *did* improve qskew (+0.20 -> +0.58) and sharpe (+0.18 -> +0.25) and pull $\beta_1$
  to ~0, but it bought skew/return, not convexity.
- **Verdict**: **kill**. Sizing is not the cap. Even when the book is made to expand
  in the crash (slow vol / sign-only), $\beta_2$ stays $\le 0$ - because the slow
  252-day signal is on the *wrong side* when fast equity-led crashes begin, so growing
  the book grows a losing position. The convexity is absent, not suppressed.

### own-gamma - is it long-gamma to its OWN universe? (diagnostic)

- **Hypothesis** (trend-true, from the user's reassertion that this is the trend pole,
  not a defensive sleeve): the SPY-benchmarked convexity gate is a crisis-alpha test;
  the real trend signature is long-gamma to the sleeve's OWN universe moves (Fung-Hsieh
  straddle is on its own instruments). Regress monthly sleeve return on its own
  universe's average absolute move; a long-gamma sleeve gains in active months, bleeds
  in quiet. Harness `/tmp/atalanta_owngamma.py`.
- **Keep/kill criteria** (pre-registered): CONFIRM trend membership if the sleeve loads
  *positively* on own-universe move-magnitude (gains in active months, > quiet months).
  KILL if it loads flat/negative (gains in quiet, loses in active = short gamma).
- **Result**: SHORT gamma. Mean monthly sleeve return by own-universe-move tercile:
  quiet **+0.55%**, mid +0.30%, active **-0.35%** - it makes the most when the book is
  calm and *loses* when the book moves. Loading slope +0.20 but t +0.28, R^2 0.001
  (no relationship). And `corr(own-universe move, SPY move) = 0.97`: on this
  equity-heavy ten-ETF book there is no separable trend axis - the book's movement IS
  equity movement.
- **Verdict**: **kill**. Atalanta is not long-gamma even to its own universe; the
  lagged slow signal is wrong-footed on the book's big moves (reversals), so high-
  movement months are losing months - the concave/short-gamma signature, the opposite
  of trend. Not fixable by re-benchmarking the gate; the construction on this substrate
  realizes short gamma.

### substrate - non-equity book + own-gamma (diagnostic)

- **Hypothesis** (the day's conclusion, now tested): family #1 is mis-substrated, not
  mis-constructed. A non-equity book (rates / FX / commodities) whose move-magnitude
  *decorrelates* from SPY will give the straddle a real trend axis and own-gamma > 0.
  Universe: SHY IEF TLT TIP HYG / UUP FXE FXY FXA / GLD SLV DBC DBA USO UNG DBB (16).
  Harnesses `/tmp/atalanta_substrate.py`, `/tmp/atalanta_owngamma_eff.py`.
- **Keep/kill criteria** (pre-registered): KEEP if (a) corr(own-move, SPY-move) falls
  well below 0.97, AND (b) the sleeve loads positively on its own trend-*efficiency*
  (gains in trending months, loses in chop) with t > 1.5. KILL if own-gamma stays
  flat/negative on the decorrelated book too.
- **Result**: KEEP. corr(own-move, SPY-move) 0.97 -> **0.52 daily** (0.82 monthly).
  $\beta_2$ vs SPY went **positive** for the first time (+0.0024 at lb252/vw21, positive
  for 6/8 candidates; equity book was -0.0028), with crash +0.17%, bear-beta -0.14,
  quarterly skew +1.28, Sharpe +0.18. The raw-magnitude own-gamma still read quiet >
  active, but that metric conflates vol with trend; conditioned on trend **efficiency**
  (directional travel / total travel) the long-gamma signature is clear and
  significant: lb252/vw21 chop **-0.63%** / mid +0.32% / trend **+0.72%** per month,
  slope t +2.20 (p 0.031); lb252/vw63 trend +0.71% vs chop -0.59% (p 0.026). Slow
  lookbacks win (lb189 was the 2/8 with $\beta_2 < 0$).
- **Verdict**: **keep**. On a non-equity substrate Atalanta is a genuine long-gamma
  trend sleeve - decorrelated from equities, convex vs SPY (Fung-Hsieh signature), and
  significantly long-gamma to its own trend efficiency. Family #1 reached.

- **Verdict**: **keep**. The non-equity trend pole is robust out of sample (own-gamma

## Day summary

The two trend-preserving rescue hypotheses both fail cleanly: Atalanta's flat
$\beta_2$-vs-SPY is neither a measurement-horizon artifact (H1) nor a sizing cap (H2).
The convexity is genuinely absent at the SPY benchmark. The triangulated cause is
signal-speed vs crash-speed on an equity-dominated small book: the slow trend signal
is mis-positioned at the onset of fast equity crashes (no crash-time convexity), and
the fast signal whipsaws (barbell already killed). This is the same wall the universe
work hit - and it is exactly the regime where a trend sleeve's *equity-crisis*
convexity is supposed to come from its **non-equity** legs (bonds/FX/commodities
trending while equities fall), which a ten-ETF book is too equity-heavy and too narrow
to supply.

The reframe that motivated the own-gamma test - that the SPY gate is a crisis-alpha
test mis-applied to trend - was itself tested and **did not rescue the sleeve**.
Re-benchmarking convexity to Atalanta's own universe still reads SHORT gamma: it gains
in quiet months and loses in active ones. And the own-universe move-magnitude is 0.97
correlated with SPY's, so on this ten-ETF book there is no separable trend axis to
re-benchmark to in the first place. The primary trend signatures Atalanta *does* show
($\beta_1 \approx 0$, positive quarterly skew) are necessary but not sufficient: a
market-neutral, right-skewed stream that is short-gamma to its own moves is a
mean-reversion/carry-shaped book wearing a trend label, not the long-gamma pole.

The triangulated conclusion across the day: the construction is not the blocker
(measurement, sizing, and benchmark all eliminated) - the **substrate** was. And the
substrate fix *worked*: moving the same v2 straddle to a non-equity book (rates / FX /
commodities) decorrelated the trend axis from SPY (0.97 -> 0.52), turned $\beta_2$ vs
SPY positive for the first time, and delivered a significant long-gamma loading on the
book's own trend efficiency. Family #1's long-gamma trend pole is **reached on the
right substrate** - the equity-ETF book was simply the wrong one (its moves are equity
reversals, not sustained cross-asset trends). The clean trend-membership test is
own-gamma to trend *efficiency*, not raw move magnitude (which conflates vol with
trend) and not SPY-convexity alone (which is the breadth-dependent crisis-alpha
byproduct).
