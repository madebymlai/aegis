# 2026-07-09

## Goal

Settle the **build-vs-buy** question for the trend seat (GH #80) that `mf_probe` opened
inside the carry campaign but never resolved on the trend seat's OWN scorecard: does
**buying a managed-futures UCITS wrapper** (iMGP DBi / DBMF, now a live UCITS ETF -
`DBMF` LSE/Xetra/Euronext, `DBMFE` EUR, 0.75% TER, 10-15 liquid futures L/S across
equities/bonds/FX/commodities) beat or hold the hand-built atalanta champion on the
#80 trend scorecard - specifically the **unmet standalone-Sharpe breadth target**
(0.97 -> 1.15 -> 1.3) that a thin 3-name convex book cannot self-source?

Why re-open a question the carry campaign leaned "don't rebuild": `mf_probe`
([[runs/demeter/2026-07-04]], `scratchpad/mf_probe.py`) judged DBMF as a **carry
partner** (composite-CE / delta_theta, ex-fast-crash), where it scored +0.0132 (the
weakest of WTMF/DBMF/KMLM; full-sample corr +0.21) and the small edge over our real
pole's +0.007 was diagnosed as **fast-crash handling = the tail sleeve's job**. It was
never judged on `trend_convexity_payoff` (the seat primary) or on standalone Sharpe (the
seat's book-Sharpe drag). This session tests exactly those two, so the answer stops being
inferred from the carry lens.

### PRE-REGISTRATION - dbmf_atalanta_blend (hybrid; run_id assigned at launch)

- **Hypothesis**: The trend seat is not build-OR-buy. A **blend** - DBMF breadth core at
  weight `w` + a thin atalanta convex dose at `(1 - w)` - **holds the convexity floor while
  lifting standalone Sharpe**, because the two poles are **complements**: atalanta supplies
  the slow-crisis (2022-type) convexity DBMF dilutes, DBMF supplies the futures-substrate
  breadth (√BR, ~10-15 markets) atalanta's 3-name book cannot. If true, both #80 scorecard
  numbers close at once (convexity `>=` champion AND standalone Sharpe `->` 1.15) with a
  single instrument added.
  - *Honest prior (pre-registered): more likely INTERPOLATION than a Pareto bulge.* DBMF and
    atalanta are both trend on overlapping macro betas, so in **trend space** they are
    positively correlated (the squaring identity, [[the-orthogonal-non-equity-trend-universe]]) -
    the blend may just slide along a line between the two endpoints (convexity falls, Sharpe
    rises monotonically in `w`) rather than bulge above it. A Pareto win requires DBMF's
    breadth to add **independent** bets atalanta genuinely lacks; the run measures whether it
    does. This is the same complements-vs-substitutes question, decided on the frontier not by
    assertion.

- **Construction**: a composite sleeve `target = w * DBMF_proxy + (1 - w) * atalanta_champion`,
  gross-normalized on the #80 harness. Nested controls: `w = 0` reproduces the atalanta
  champion **byte-exact** (the convexity anchor); `w = 1` reproduces the standalone
  `dbmf_seatchallenger` **byte-exact** (the breadth anchor). Only `w` is swept, so mu-noise
  stays off the ranker. **Depends on `dbmf_seatchallenger` running first** - the blend is only
  interpretable once DBMF's standalone family-membership and its own convexity/Sharpe are
  measured (else a blend cell cannot be attributed to core vs dose).

- **Keep/kill criteria** (pre-registered, before launching; same champion reference and #80
  harness as the block above; judged on HELD-OUT):
  - **Sweep**: `w` in {0.0, 0.25, 0.50, 0.75, 1.0}. The two endpoints are nested controls,
    reproduced to the digit, or the run is invalid.
  - **(a) Family membership** verifies across every active blend cell (positive own-horizon
    quarterly skew / convex shape). A cell that fails is disqualified for the seat.
  - **(b) The Pareto bar (the whole test).** There exists an **interior** `w` (0 < w < 1)
    where `trend_convexity_payoff >= 0.4451 - CI` (convexity floor HELD) **AND** standalone
    Sharpe `>= 1.15` by `> CI` (the seat target reached) **AND** the 2022-type slow crisis
    stays `>= +4.5%` (the slow-crisis convexity atalanta supplies survives the DBMF weight).
    That cell is the "buy the breadth, keep a thin convex core" champion.
  - **(c) Guardrail floors held** at that cell: worst calm year `>= -6%`,
    `|monthly corr to carry| <= 0.10`, worst-split maxDD `<=` champion + 2pp.
  - **Verdict rule**:
    - **KEEP (hybrid champion)** if an interior `w` clears (b) + (c): the poles are
      **complements**, the seat becomes DBMF-core + thin-atalanta-dose, and both scorecard
      numbers close together.
    - **KILL as substitutes** if `trend_convexity_payoff` falls **monotonically** in `w` and
      every Sharpe-lifting cell breaches the convexity floor (interpolation, no bulge): the
      poles are substitutes, pick ONE, the seat stays pure atalanta and DBMF is not for it.

- **What changed since previous run**: first **composite** construction of the trend seat -
  a bought breadth beta blended with the hand-built convex sleeve, testing complementarity
  rather than replacement.

- **Scope note - the "tail sleeve" overlay flavor is NOT this block.** The tail tier
  (`aegis.tailOverlay`, [[runs/aegis/2026-07-04]]) restores only **fast**-crash (2020-type)
  convexity and already sits at the book level by roster design; it does not fill the
  **slow**-crisis (2022-type) gap a DBMF core opens, which is what a thin **atalanta** dose
  fills. So this block tests the atalanta-dose flavor (the slow-crisis restorer); the tail
  tier stays a separate book-level seat, not a trend-seat overlay.

### volbeta (vol-scaling exponent sweep - closes the sizing axis)

- **Hypothesis**: de-emphasizing the high-vol (crisis-paying) legs lifts convexity - the champion
  divides leg magnitude by `realized_vol` (vol_beta=1); sweeping the exponent down should weight the
  crisis legs more as their vol spikes (Kaminski-Hoffman "Taming of the Skew": inverse-vol / constant
  -risk sizing has the lowest skew). `atalanta.keystone_volbeta`, vol_beta {0,0.25,0.5,0.75,1.0},
  everything else pinned to champion; vol_beta=1 is the nested control.
  - *Honest prior (pre-registered): LOW-MEDIUM, expected ~null.* Carver's decisive test - reducing
    vol-targeting moves SKEW but not the RIGHT tail, and our metric is right-minus-left-tail. Also on
    this gross-normalized FORGO book vol appears in both numerator and gross divisor, so vol_beta only
    re-weights the cross-sectional split, not the full time-series ERT mechanism (that would be the
    tail sleeve's equity-linked job).
- **Keep/kill** (pre-registered in `configs/atalanta/trend_volbeta.yaml`): KEEP if some vol_beta != 1
  clears `trend_convexity_payoff >= 0.447` by > CI AND holds the calm-year / maxDD guardrails; KILL if
  convexity is flat/declining or any lift is within CI.
