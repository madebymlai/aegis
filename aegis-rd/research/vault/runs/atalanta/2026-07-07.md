### atalanta_tailparity

- **Hypothesis** (GH #80, [[budgeting-the-divergent-seat]] H1 - the off-sleeve convexity lever after
  pyramiding closed): sizing the champion's existing legs by inverse EXPECTED SHORTFALL (tail-risk
  parity) instead of inverse VOL lowers the floor sleeve's worst-split drawdown at convexity parity-
  or-better, because equalizing TAIL risk down-weights fat-left-tail legs the vol-parity sizing over-
  weights. Re-weights existing legs only (instrument-orthogonality safe). New indicator
  atalanta.realized_shortfall (annualized rolling ES, worst decile of 126d, sqrt(252)-scaled); new
  strategy atalanta.keystone_tailparity nests the champion at tilt=0 (risk = vol exactly); tilt swept
  {0, 0.33, 0.66, 1.0}.
- **Design note** (Exa, "do exa search for proper design"): chose the ROBUST self-contained tail-
  parity over the faithful-but-fragile equity-crisis-conditional tilt. The asymmetric-correlation
  literature (Ang-Chen 2002; Campbell-Koedijk-Kofman 2008; Hong-Tu-Zhou) is blunt that exceedance /
  conditional correlations carry a CONDITIONING BIAS and have poor finite-sample properties with few
  tail observations - fatal on 3 legs and ~2-3 crises. The bias-robust route is to condition on the
  PORTFOLIO tail (CVaR risk contribution; VaR/ES-implied correlation), and a MODERATE alpha (worst
  decile, not a 1% exceedance) keeps the estimate data-efficient. Inverse-ES sizing is exactly that:
  a portfolio-level, data-efficient tail measure. The equity-conditioned downside-beta version (the
  literal "overlay capacity") is the follow-up IF this shows signal - it needs an SPY covariate side-
  loaded into the sizing path (real plumbing) and is the fragile one, so robustness-first was chosen.
  TRP backing: AllianceBernstein "An Introduction to Tail Risk Parity"; Boudt et al. 2013; the ERC-ES
  thesis (lower realized MaxDD/downside-vol than vol-ERC, at higher turnover - hence the fee gate).
- **Keep/kill criteria** (pre-registered BEFORE launching; champion = in-grid tilt=0 cell, ~0.4451
  conv / ~10.18% worst maxDD / ~1.15 Sharpe):
  - **H1 earns its keep**: >= 1 active tilt cell PARETO-improves the tilt=0 champion - held-out
    worst-split max_dd strictly LOWER at trend_convexity_payoff no worse (within noise), OR convexity
    strictly higher at max_dd no worse - with a smooth profile across tilt.
  - **H2 own-thesis sanity**: worst-split max_dd must not RISE with tilt (inverse-ES raising the tail
    would contradict tail parity's premise); UPI-style efficiency not collapsing.
  - **H3 fee/turnover gate**: total_fees / trade count not materially above the champion's (TRP is a
    higher-turnover sizing; the EUR 5k / 1.25 fixed-fee floor is the destsweep hazard).
  - **KEEP**: an active cell clears H1 without an H3 fee blowup. **KILL**: no active cell improves
    convexity-per-drawdown over the champion, OR max_dd rises with tilt, OR fees explode. HONEST
    PRIOR: the 3 curated legs are already inverse-vol-sized and may be near-Gaussian enough that
    ES ~ vol x const, making the tilt near-inert - a marginal/null "tail parity ~ vol parity on this
    book" result is the likely, still-informative outcome. No tolerance-tuning.
- **What changed since previous run**: a different lever entirely - not pyramiding (settled) but the
  SIZING denominator; the signal, latch, short mute, bands, universe and gross are the champion's, and
  the single swept axis is the inverse-vol -> inverse-ES blend.
- **Result** (run `20260706T222912371847Z_atalanta_tailparity`, held-out means over 6 splits; tilt=1.0
  dropped by best/median/worst role selection but its direction is inferable from the monotone trend):

  | tilt | conv | worst maxDD | Sharpe | fees | vs champion |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 0.0 (champion) | 0.4451 | 10.18% | 1.147 | 23.8 | nested control (reproduced exactly) |
  | 0.33 | 0.4353 | 10.17% | 1.122 | 23.8 | conv -0.0098, dd -0.01pp |
  | 0.66 | 0.4303 | 10.20% | 1.113 | 24.1 | conv -0.0149, dd +0.01pp |

  The tilt=0 cell reproduced the champion to the digit (conv 0.4451 / worst maxDD 10.18%), confirming
  the nesting. As sizing moves toward inverse-ES, held-out convexity DROPS monotonically while worst-
  split maxDD is FLAT (10.17-10.20%, within +/-0.01pp = noise) and fees/turnover barely move (H3 pass,
  no fill storm). So no active cell Pareto-improves the champion: the tilt buys no survivability and
  costs a little convexity.
- **Verdict**: **kill** (pre-registered; the honest prior realized). H1 fail (no Pareto gain - worst
  maxDD flat, convexity down), H2 vacuous (dd does not fall, the mechanism's own thesis does not fire),
  H3 pass. Tail parity ~ vol parity on THIS book, for a mechanism reason worth banking: the three legs
  are curated + already inverse-vol-sized and near-Gaussian enough in the left tail that ES ~ vol x a
  near-common constant, so under gross-normalization the tilt barely re-weights them - and where it
  does, it slightly starves the convexity-carrying leg. More important, the worst-split drawdown did
  not move AT ALL with any sizing tilt, because this sleeve's worst drawdowns are a SIGNAL/WHIPSAW
  property (the latch caught wrong-footed in a reversal), not a leg-sizing property - no re-weighting
  of 3 legs' tail-vs-vol risk can touch a drawdown the trend signal itself creates. This is the same
  lesson the pyramid arc taught from the other side (the drawdown is intrinsic to the trend exposure),
  now confirmed on the sizing axis. Champion (lock 20260629T000858802034Z) stands. The equity-crisis-
  conditional "overlay capacity" follow-up is DOWNGRADED: this null undercuts its premise (if a tail-
  risk sizing tilt cannot move the drawdown, an equity-conditioned one is unlikely to either, and it
  carries far more estimation fragility) - not worth its plumbing until a different lever moves the
  needle. No tolerance-tuning; tilt=1.0 not probed (monotone decline cannot yield a KEEP).

### atalanta_trend_meaningful

- **Hypothesis** (GH #80, the SIGNAL-SOTA lever, prompted by "are the indicators optimal - do a
  best-practice search"): the champion signals on the RAW annualized OLS slope, which reports a
  trend's direction and steepness but ignores how well the line FITS. State-of-the-art
  "significance-aware" trend signals - Baltas & Kosowski's TREND (the t-statistic of the slope) and
  Bryhn & Dimberg's "statistically meaningful trend" (trade only when R^2 >= 0.65) - normalize the
  slope by the fit's noise, standing aside in low-R^2 chop. Thesis: down-weighting the slope in
  low-R^2 regimes lifts convexity-per-fill by cutting the trendless-winter whipsaw (2023) while
  leaving the clean high-R^2 crisis trends untouched. New indicator atalanta.trend_score_meaningful
  weights the champion score by `R^2 ** significance` (R^2 of the SAME log-price-on-time regression),
  a continuous, unit-preserving form that keeps the enter/exit bands + magnitude response intact; a
  pure signal swap (keystone_shortmute, vol sizing, legs, portfolio all the champion's). significance
  swept {0.0, 0.5, 1.0, 2.0}; 0.0 reproduces the champion slope byte-for-byte (code-branched).
- **Design note** (Exa SOTA sweep of the indicator stack): the sweep also SETTLED the other two
  choices, so only the signal was tested. VOL estimator (ctc 126d): confirmed SOTA-adequate - four
  sources (Jalevik 2024; Baltas-Kosowski; Fan-Li-Liu; the CME TSMOM paper) agree sophisticated vol
  models (GARCH/HAR-RV/YZ) buy only marginal risk-adjusted gain over realized vol, the gain is
  TURNOVER not Sharpe, and "higher forecast accuracy does not translate to portfolio performance" -
  the campaign already killed YZ/EWMA and vindicated ctc. Signal FORM (regression slope): Levine-
  Pedersen ("Which Trend Is Your Friend") prove OLS trend = TSMOM = MACD/EWMA-crossover = Kalman, all
  linear filters R^2>80% to each other - the slope is a valid SOTA family member, not inferior. The
  only untested SOTA gap was the slope's SIGNIFICANCE weighting. Soft R^2-weight chosen over the SMT
  HARD gate (R^2>=0.65 else flat): a 0.65 cliff + full abstention is brittle and kin to the killed
  turngate/memgate family (flat-then-miss the re-acceleration) on a thin 3-leg book.
- **Keep/kill criteria** (pre-registered BEFORE launching; champion = in-grid significance=0.0 cell,
  held-out conv ~0.4451 / worst-split maxDD ~10.18% / ~8 fills / ~24 fees):
  - **H1 earns its keep**: >= 1 active cell (gamma>0) PARETO-improves the gamma=0 champion - convexity
    strictly higher at worst-split maxDD no worse, OR fills/fees materially lower at convexity + maxDD
    no worse - with a smooth (non-spec-luck) profile across gamma.
  - **H2 turnover claim (the SOTA promise)**: fills/fees fall as gamma rises (the signal trades less
    noise); a flat or rising fill count refutes the mechanism.
  - **H3 regime diagnostic**: the gain concentrates in the trendless splits (2023 winter), not a
    uniform down-trade across all splits (a uniform effect = the R^2 weight merely raised the enter
    band). HONEST PRIOR (Baltas-Kosowski): signal choice moves TURNOVER far more than pre-cost
    performance - the likely win, if any, is a fee reduction at convexity parity, not a convexity
    miracle. **KEEP**: an active cell clears H1 with H2 support. **KILL**: no active cell Pareto-beats
    champion, OR turnover does not fall (H2 fail), OR the gain is uniform under-trading (H3 fail). No
    tolerance-tuning.
- **What changed since previous run**: the SIGNAL lever - distinct from the response-function
  (pyramid), sizing (tail parity), and universe (drop-one-in) levers already closed today; the slope
  is weighted by its own fit quality, everything else byte-identical to the champion.
- **Result** (run `20260706T225637843391Z_atalanta_trend_meaningful`, held-out means over 6 splits;
  gamma=1.0 dropped by best/median/worst role selection of 4; 0 degenerate / 0 invalid):
