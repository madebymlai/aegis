# 2026-07-10

## Goal

Close the last open point in the sizing axis: the UNLEVERED power response. Two Exa rounds
([[sizing-shape-is-already-convexity-maximal]]) established that the champion already makes every
convexity-maximising construction choice (proportional, uncapped, single-smoother, long vol window -
CFM / Dao-Bouchaud), and that the one lever never run is scaling MORE than proportionally into strong
trends. The three killed pyramids all did this by EXTENSION (P&L ramp) - either adding a lag or adding
2x financed gross. The power-on-score response (`conv ** pyr_power` before `/vol`, gross-renormalised)
is the same super-proportionality with NEITHER: no lag, no leverage. Does convex response at constant
gross lift held-out convexity within the floor's survivability envelope?

  side. Champion (linear response) stands as the parsimonious pinned incumbent (the separability
  warning's explicit recommendation). Fourth kill of the pyramid/response super-proportionality family.

### 20260710T100358260860Z_atalanta_trend_ewmac

- **Hypothesis** (the second-moment filter-swap question, GH #80): Levine-Pedersen prove the EWMA
  crossover and the OLS slope are the same LINEAR-FILTER class - equivalent on the SIGNAL (first
  moment) - but equivalence is silent on turnover/lag/whipsaw (second moment), which drive Sharpe
  and drawdown. The two differ in weight SHAPE (OLS weights the window CENTER most, EWMAC weights
  RECENT returns most). Does swapping ONLY the estimator (slope -> causal Carver EWMAC, units and
  everything else held byte-identical) keep convexity while improving Sharpe/maxDD - the "different
  filter that fits us better" the user asked for? Two Exa rounds pre-registered the prior: Baltas-
  Kosowski show the linear-trend FIT minimises turnover (so the slope is already the net-Sharpe-
  efficient filter); Grebenkov single-EMA is Sharpe-optimal and MACD does not beat it; Carver's own
  skew-by-speed table has slow crossovers carrying NEGATIVE daily skew -> expect tie-or-lose.
- **Keep/kill** (pre-registered in [[../../configs/atalanta/trend_ewmac.yaml]]): H1 best EWMAC speed
  BEATS champion convexity 0.4183 with a non-degenerate (significant) profile; H2 worst-split
  max_dd <= 1.5x champion 10.18; SECONDARY the real axis - any speed Pareto-improves Sharpe (1.323)
  or mean maxDD (6.61) at tied convexity. KILL if no speed beats convexity AND none Pareto-improves
  Sharpe/maxDD.
- **What changed**: new indicator `atalanta.trend_score_ewmac` v1.0.0 - causal EWMAC (EMA(logP,
  Lfast) - EMA(logP, Lslow), Lslow=4*Lfast, recursive/no-lookahead) rescaled by the crossover
  horizon (Lslow-Lfast)/2 into the SAME annualized-return units as the OLS slope (verified to match
  it to 4dp on a steady trend), so `keystone_shortmute` + bands + short_cap consume it UNCHANGED and
  the swap is single-variable. NOT vol-normalised in the indicator (the strategy already divides by
  realized_vol; normalising twice = pysystemtrade #102). Lslow loop-edited {126, 189, 252} (same
  protocol/grid as trend_speedsweep; run_ids 20260710T100453824407Z (126) and 20260710T100529531943Z
  (252)).
- **Result** (held-out `trend_convexity_payoff`, 7 splits):

  | filter | conv | Sharpe | mean maxDD | worst-split maxDD | fees (turnover) |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | champion OLS slope lb189 | 0.4183 | 1.323 | 6.61 | 10.18 | 23.6 |
  | EWMAC Lslow=126 | 0.4014 | 1.231 | 6.90 | 9.17 | 33.2 |
  | EWMAC Lslow=189 (matched) | 0.4159 | 1.076 | 10.15 | 10.15 | 25.9 |
  | EWMAC Lslow=252 | 0.4311 | 1.129 | 7.76 | 11.52 | 17.2 |

  At the MATCHED horizon (189) EWMAC ties-to-slightly-loses convexity (0.4159 vs 0.4183) - the
  equivalence holding as predicted. The lone cell that edges champion convexity, Lslow=252 (0.4311,
  +0.013), is NOT real: paired against champion per-split it is Wilcoxon p=0.81 / paired-t p=0.90,
  the +0.013 driven entirely by ONE split (s3 0.952 vs 0.485) while EWMAC is worse on 5 of 7 - the

- **Verdict**: **KILL**. H1 fails the significance clause (the 252 edge is split noise). The SECONDARY
  axis - the user's actual question - fails decisively: EWMAC loses Sharpe at ALL three speeds
  (1.231 / 1.076 / 1.129, all < champion 1.323 and the slow cell below the sleeve's own 1.15 floor)
  and loses drawdown at the matched and slow speeds; it never Pareto-improves. Mechanism, exactly
  Baltas-Kosowski confirmed on our trio: the crossover's RECENT-weighting is noisier at the endpoint
  than the OLS slope's CENTER-weighting, so it flips more -> higher turnover (fees up to 33 at the
  fast end) -> worse net Sharpe and deeper drawdown for the SAME convexity. So the second moment the
  user rightly flagged DOES differ across filters - and it differs in the CHAMPION'S favour. The OLS
  slope is not only convexity-optimal but also the turnover/Sharpe/DD-efficient filter. Filter-swap
  axis closed by measurement.

## Day summary

The sizing axis is now closed by MEASUREMENT on all four faces: vol-exponent (`volbeta`, flat),
extension pyramiding (levered add-on + give-back stop, convexity real but survivability-breaking),
tail-parity leg sizing (null), and now the UNLEVERED power response (convexity gain not real on a thin
book). The champion's linear, proportional, gross-normalised response is the convexity-optimal sizing
here - not by prior, by the grid. Above the ~0.45 harvest ceiling needs BOUGHT gamma (tail sleeve),
not a re-shaped harvest.

Second, the TREND-FILTER-SWAP axis is now also closed by measurement. Prompted by the sharp point
that Levine-Pedersen equivalence is only a first-moment (signal) statement and says nothing about
the second moment (turnover/lag/whipsaw -> Sharpe/DD), a causal Carver EWMAC was built as a clean
single-variable swap for the OLS slope and swept over speed. It ties-to-loses convexity at matched
horizon and LOSES Sharpe at every speed (and drawdown at the matched/slow speeds), because its
recent-weighting is noisier at the endpoint and trades more. The second moment does differ across
filters - in the champion's favour (Baltas-Kosowski confirmed): the OLS slope is the turnover/
Sharpe/DD-efficient filter, not just the convexity-optimal one. Signal, formula, filter-estimator,
speed, sizing (four faces), universe, and build-vs-buy are now all closed on the atalanta trio.
