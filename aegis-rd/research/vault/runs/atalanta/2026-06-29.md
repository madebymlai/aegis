# 2026-06-29

## Goal

Test a **slow-fast turning-point gate** on the live champion ([[trend-following-in-whipsaw-regimes]],
new hypothesis from the 2026-06 literature pass; primary source Harvey, Goulding & Mazzoleni,
"Breaking Bad Trends", FAJ 2023). The live walk-forward of `trend.yaml` has one ugly out-of-sample
split - return **-5.4%**, Sharpe **-0.53**, win **20%**, convexity **0.026** - against +25-27% in the
good windows. That is not a crisis failure (the tail sleeve's job); it is a **turning-point / whipsaw
window** - the slow sleeve giving its convexity back at a reversal the sticky hysteretic latch holds
through. The gate de-risks a name toward cash when a **fast** signal has already turned but the slow
latch has not. This is the fast signal as a **gate, never a trading leg** - the reconciliation of the
2026-06-10 fast-leg kill (a fast *position* buys its own whipsaw on a thin book; a fast *modulator*
on the slow position does not).

### 20260628T221359891740Z_atalanta_trend_turngate (turning-point gate sweep)

- **Hypothesis**: a fast signal used as a turning-point gate on the slow leg (scale the position
  toward cash on fast/slow sign disagreement), not as an additive trading leg, reduces the
  turning-point bleed without re-introducing the whipsaw the killed fast leg added.
- **Keep/kill criteria** (pre-registered, before launching):

    grid); (2) the **worst** held-out split improves vs the champion (the -5.4% / -0.53-Sharpe
    window lifts); (3) the **2022 inflation-crisis** split convexity is **not diluted** (stays at or
    above the champion's on that split).
  - **KILL** if no cell beats the champion on held-out convexity, OR every improvement comes by
    flattening the crisis split (muting the convex entry - the same trap that killed the downward
    slow-ensemble for the floor pole), OR the gate buys only smooth-window Sharpe at a convexity cost.
  - **Change-my-mind / diagnostic**: if the worst split's loss is **trend-strength** driven (fast and
    slow stay *aligned* through it) rather than a reversal, the gate is mis-targeted -> fall back to
    the RA vol-window sweep (`vol_window` 126 -> 252/378) instead.

  book: a fast gate, even as a *modulator* and not a trading leg, still forfeits convex capture on
  false-positive pullbacks about as often as it saves a true reversal, exactly the AQR "can't filter
  your way out" null ([[trend-following-in-whipsaw-regimes]]). The change-my-mind clause did NOT fire
  (split 4 *is* a reversal - the gate improved it - not trend-strength), but the lever fails on the
  hired objective regardless. **Pivot**: the gate is a *drawdown* tool, not a convexity tool (it cut
  the worst-window and mean drawdown at `gate=0.5`), so it belongs to the one-sided-mute / drawdown
  family, never the convex pole.

### 20260628T222637060832Z_atalanta_trend_shortmute (one-sided negative mute sweep)

  book's only short leg - compressing the deepest short conviction toward cash while leaving the longs
  uncapped, cuts held-out drawdown without flattening the longs. The asymmetry is the point: capping
  the positive tail would trade away convexity; compressing the negative extreme is drawdown control.
- **Keep/kill criteria** (pre-registered, before launching):
  - **KEEP** if some `short_cap` cuts held-out drawdown (mean max_dd AND the worst split's
    max_dd/return) vs the `short_cap=1.0` champion control, WHILE held-out `trend_convexity_payoff`

    diluted.

    drawdown cut comes from muting the IDTL short in 2022 - the deepest IDTL downtrend, when the short
    is largest AND most profitable (the drawdown-payer's purpose year) - diluting the crisis split.
    That confirms the short leg IS the crisis-payoff leg and cannot be muted cheaply (the gate's
    crisis-dilution lesson, on the short side).
  - **Change-my-mind**: if a cap cuts drawdown with convexity intact AND 2022 preserved, the mute is a

## Day summary

Two pre-registered tests on the live champion, both with bit-for-bit champion controls, and the
**contrast between them is the lesson**.

1. **Turning-point GATE (Harvey "Breaking Bad Trends" FAJ 2023) - KILLED on convexity.** A fast read
   gating the slow position to cash on fast/slow disagreement *correctly targets* the worst split (it
   really is a reversal: it lifts split 4 -5.35% -> -2.81%) but is convexity-*negative* in aggregate
   (held-out 0.424 -> 0.401 -> 0.358 as the gate strengthens) because, being symmetric, it also
   de-risks healthy *long* pullbacks. The AQR filter-null reproduced on our own book.
2. **One-sided short MUTE (Invesco) - CONFIRMED, the session's keeper.** Capping only the IDTL short's
   deepest readings (longs untouched, freed gross -> cash) beats the champion, and the confirmation
   sweep (finer caps + UPI + crisis-conditional) brackets an **interior peak at `short_cap ~= 0.05`**:
   convexity 0.424 -> 0.453 (+6.8%), UPI 6.21 -> 7.02 (+13%), mean drawdown 8.58 -> 6.51, the worst
   whipsaw split's drawdown crushed 13.45 -> 2.82. The one counter-current: `crisis_quarter_return`
   *decreases* as the cap tightens (0.287 -> 0.273 -> 0.262), so the IDTL short does add crisis-
   conditional return at full size, and the cap trades ~5% of it for the convexity/UPI/drawdown gains.
   Long-only (`0.0`) is worse than `0.05` on both convexity and crisis return, so the lever is "cap
   the short's whipsaw-prone deep conviction", not "drop the short".

The mechanism contrast is the day's lesson: a **symmetric** filter that touches the longs forfeits
convex capture on false positives (the killed gate); a **one-sided** mute that touches only the
short's left-tail whipsaw improves the live objective, UPI and drawdown at a small crisis-return cost
(the kept mute). The mute is promote-ready; the only open call is the cap value (0.05 = max
convexity/UPI; 0.075-0.10 = keep more crisis return) and whether the ~5% crisis give-back is acceptable
for a drawdown-payer sleeve.
