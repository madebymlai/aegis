**Decay literature - which leverage wins for a multi-week trend-gated hold** (web pass, 2026-07-03):
the physics is settled and unanimous. Volatility drag ~= `leverage^2 * 1/2 * variance` (a 3x on 20%
vol flat loses ~9%/yr vs ~2% for -1x), and higher moments scale f^2/f^3/f^4 (a -3x carries 9x the
variance drag, 27x the skew and 81x the kurtosis risk of a -1x).[Alpha in Academia, QuantDecoded]
Trainor (2013): -1x is holdable **months-to-a-year** in normal vol; -2x/-3x only **1-3 months**, and
in high-vol crises even -2x/-3x collapse to **~19 days**. A trend gate helps (sustained one-way moves
make daily compounding *add* to an inverse fund's return[REX Shares]) but cannot rescue -3x, because
bond selloffs are **high-vol** regimes where the f^2 drag is worst *exactly in 2022*, and gated -3x
rate strategies still suffer 3+ year chop drawdowns[Lay Quant]. Decisive ordering on the decay axis:

> **borrow-gated IDTL short (decay-free) > -1x inverse ETF (DXSV) > -3x inverse ETF (UL3S/3TYS).**

A genuine borrow short has **zero** volatility decay - only borrow cost - and is holdable
indefinitely at the exact underlying; every inverse ETF pays daily-reset decay **plus** negative
carry (coupon + financing).[KoalaGains/TBF] The champion's hysteretic latch holds for weeks-to-months
(~8 fills/yr), a horizon on which -3x is structurally broken and -1x is fine.

**Verdict: PARK the inverse-ETF short leg; the borrow-gated IDTL short remains champion.** The `-1x`
thesis is not refuted - it is *superseded*: (1) the clean `-1x` US-Treasury UCITS the article assumed
is **extinct and uncarried**, so it is not realizable live; (2) even if it were, a real borrow short
**dominates it on decay** (decay-free, exact underlying, holdable), so the inverse ETF is only ever a
*borrow-loss fallback*; (3) the only tradable UCITS `-1x` on the account is **DXSV**, on a
**substituted EUR-sovereign curve**, not US Treasury; (4) the tradable US-Treasury inverse lines are
`-3x` (UL3S/3TYS), which the decay literature rules out for a multi-week hold. The 2026-06 IBKR probe
found IDTL **borrowable**, so the champion's short instrument is already the best available
realization. Component `atalanta.keystone_inverseshort` + `trend_inverseshort.yaml` are staged and
valid; the sole live use for them would be a **DXSV `-1x` EUR-sovereign** fallback IF IDTL borrow is
ever lost - swap the instrument id (`DXSV.IBIS2`) and re-pair, then run.

### Component built: atalanta.variance_ratio (h54 memory gate)

`research/components/indicators/atalanta/atalanta_variance_ratio.py`, id `atalanta.variance_ratio`.
Rolling Lo-MacKinlay (1988) overlapping VR(k) per symbol, params `vr_horizon` (k) / `vr_window` (W);
VR>1 = positive autocorrelation (trend persists), VR<1 = mean reversion (whipsaw-prone). A pure

how `trend_score`/`realized_vol` are pure readings. NB the adaptation is TIME-SERIES per-leg (we hold
2 commodity names), not Mehlitz-Auer's cross-sectional selection.

textbook overlapping VR (maxdiff ~1e-15 vs a brute-force per-window recompute); (2) trending series ->
VR>1, mean-reverting -> VR<1; (3) empirical VR matches the **analytic AR(1)** VR to <0.02; (4) warmup
NaN semantics correct; (5) registers cleanly as `atalanta.variance_ratio`. Neutral-level calibration:
the rolling random-walk neutral is ~1.00 at the pinned default (k=21, W=252), drifting to ~0.99 (short
W) / ~1.02 (k=42 or W>=504) - documented in the component so a gate thresholds on the MEASURED neutral,
not a naive 1.0.

**Real-data smoke (green light for h54):** on the cached legs, VR(21,252) discriminates regimes as the

mean-reversion), so the gate threshold must target the mean-reverting TAIL (VR < ~0.8), not a naive
<1.0, else it over-mutes - a swept strategy param.
