### crisis_quarter_return - building and stabilizing a crisis-conditional metric (methodology, no sweep)

Deep EXA-sourced review (academic + practitioner) of how to measure the sleeve's gamma/skew as a number
we can trust, after the held-out quarterly skew above proved to be noise. Measured on the Atalanta
long-only book (and the L/S / L/S-pyramid books) over 2018-2024:

- **Moment skew is a horizon artifact and per-split noise.** The same long-only book reads skew -0.28
  daily, +2.20 weekly, +1.08 monthly, +2.48 quarterly; per-split quarterly skew has std about 1.16, as
  large as the signal. Not trustworthy at any single frequency, and unrankable per-split. L-moments /
  medcouple are the robust *moment* replacements (lowest RMSE; 25% breakdown), but a moment is the wrong
  tool for ranking a convex sleeve at all.
- **The daily crash metric inverts sign.** `crash_day_return` (worst-decile *days*) is about -29 bps for
  the long-only book: it co-crashes on fast shocks (COVID) because trend lags. Symmetric Treynor-Mazuy
  convexity (beta_2) is about 0 - a long-only FORGO sleeve is a *one-sided* downside payer, no right-tail gamma.
- **The multi-month metric reads the real property - but the first cut was knob-unstable.** A
  worst-quintile mean over overlapping 3-month windows reads the slow, 2022-style drawdown-payer payoff
  the daily metric misses (long-only positive; L/S a bit higher - it can short the crash). But the value
  swung ~20x (+20 to +413 bps) across defensible horizon/quantile choices - the hard quantile and single
  horizon are free knobs. **Reimplemented** with a *smooth* mean-shortfall downside weight (no quantile
  cutoff) and a *2-6 month horizon-band average* (annualized to a common scale): the design spread
  collapses 3-7x (long-only 11.0 to 1.5 %/yr) while the cross-book ordering holds (long-only about
  +5%/yr, L/S/pyr about +10%). A conditional *mean* (not a third moment) is what makes it split-stable.

Shipped as the opt-in `crisis_quarter_return` custom Metric (smooth mean-shortfall downside weight, 2-6
month horizon band, annualized), the horizon-resolved sibling of `crash_day_return`; convexity unit tests
extended, mypy clean, full suite green. The bootstrap lower bound is exposed as a post-hoc helper
(`crisis_payoff_bootstrap_ci_from_curve`, seeded) - too heavy and per-split-degenerate to be the engine
ranking metric, but the right way to rank a final shortlist. Written into
[[verifying-strategy-family-membership]] (the metric-family article) and [[measuring-crisis-alpha]] (the
robust-estimator argument). The block-bootstrap CI is wide and straddles zero (long-only +5%/yr point,
roughly [-10, +27] %/yr) because only ~2 real crises sit in 2018-2024 - so the metric is a sign/ordering
signal, not a magnitude; only adding crisis information (pooling across the universe, EVT, longer history)
shrinks that. Also reverted the `tail_penalized_upi` misstep - penalizing the income engine's structural
tail is self-defeating (it is paid for that tail; the hedge is the paired long-gamma sleeve).
