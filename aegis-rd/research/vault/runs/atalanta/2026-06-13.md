## Goal

Breadth-saturation test for the orthogonal non-equity universe
([[the-orthogonal-non-equity-trend-universe]]). Hold the locked champion fixed
(`atalanta.trendStraddle`, lb252 / vw63 / eb0.0, direction both) and vary ONLY the

not an `aerd` Candidate Store run - so the universe is the only moving part.

    nominal-breadth intuition: the Tier 0 essentials (rates / gold / dollar / broad

    less-orthogonal, more equity-correlated names (credit HYG/LQD/BKLN/ANGL/EMB, EM

    $\beta_2$ (6/13) - so 20 names is the robustness sweet spot, not 10.

  The report's "stop at ~25" is directionally right (more is not better) but the
  realized optimum is lower and sharper: ~20 names (essentials + first expansion).
  The refinement tiers (25-40) dilute convexity and Sharpe and raise equity
  correlation. Extending to 35/40 confirmed the decline continues with no late
  recovery, which is exactly what going past 30 was meant to check.

## Day summary

The breadth-saturation diagnostic refines the article's plateau prior into a sharper
result: trend quality peaks at the 20-name Tier 1 and degrades thereafter, rather
than plateauing at 25. The realized orthogonality lever runs *backwards* past the
essentials core - the marginal "refinement" names are more equity-correlated than the
rates/gold/dollar/commodity core, so adding them raises corr-to-SPY and erodes the
convex tail. The locked champion's 16-asset book sits in the favorable ~20-name
regime. Net: do not expand the champion to the refinement tiers; the orthogonal core
plus first expansion (~20) is the design the data supports. The formal per-tier aerd
grids (24 candidates x 5 held-out splits each, re-optimized by Sharpe) independently
confirm the peak: best-by-Sharpe held-out Sharpe tops out at Tier 1 (+0.152) and
declines past 25, and re-optimization cannot rescue the wider universes (every tier
keeps lb252; the champion params win at the peak).
