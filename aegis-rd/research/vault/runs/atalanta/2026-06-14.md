# 2026-06-14

## Goal

Make the floor's **trend / momentum long-gamma pole** (atalanta, family #1) more
**regime-robust** by reshaping its *construction*, then validate by re-running the same
experiments the floor work uses (loose-param long-window floor + the Trend-family membership
gates, including the **own-gamma** primary test, over **both** windows). The trend pole is no
longer a rescue — the 2026-06-13/14 floor work established it is genuinely convex on the right
(non-equity) substrate — so this is **optimization, not rescue**: lift the modest long-window
Sharpe and/or deepen the convexity *while keeping the long-gamma signature*.

The incoming prior (external exa literature sweep — Etienne et al. 2025 arXiv:2510.23150,
Goulding-Harvey 2023 JFE, AQR "You Can't Always Trend" 2019, CFM "Convexity of Trend
Following", Man-AHL) pointed hardest at a **fast+slow barbell** (drop the redundant medium).
Our own vault pointed the *opposite* way: the barbell's fast leg is already in the graveyard
(2026-06-10 v6, fast leg added whipsaw) — but that was on the **wrong (equity/thin) substrate**,
so the question "does a barbell work on the *right* non-equity book?" was still open. This run
settles it and finds the construction lever that actually works.

Champion under test: `atalanta.trendStraddle` v2 — `trend_score` lb252 + `realized_vol` vw63 +
`trendStraddle` eb0.0 — on the live 20-name Tier-1 London book (`atalanta/trend.yaml`,
held-out +0.571). Substrate substrate-requirement and gates: [[verifying-strategy-family-membership]];
upgrade path "decompose sub-roles by horizon": [[the-tiered-strategy-roster]]; whipsaw priors:
[[trend-following-in-whipsaw-regimes]], [[relative-vs-absolute-momentum-in-small-universes]];
breadth: [[the-orthogonal-non-equity-trend-universe]].

### 2 — slow-band ensemble (adjacent slow lookbacks, NO fast leg) — WINNER

**Keep/kill (pre-registered):** CONFIRM if averaging *adjacent slow* lookbacks improves the
pole on the convexity axis (qskew / crisis-conditional / drawdown / own-gamma) at ≈flat Sharpe.
Mechanism is **lookback specification-luck diversification**, not Sharpe alpha: a single spec
is fragile (the [[relative-vs-absolute-momentum-in-small-universes]] note: one parameter notch
swung a published dual-momentum backtest 2,151 bp), and adjacent slow horizons are ~0.84–0.90
correlated, so averaging them keeps the trend while smoothing the spec noise.

better counterparty to the short-gamma carry (it stops being "trend-too-strong", the

## Verdict — KEPT (slow-band ensemble), barbell graveyard confirmed

Reshaping the trend pole **works — but the lever is the opposite of the incoming prior.** A
fast+slow barbell is refuted on the right substrate too (definitively in the graveyard now);
the robust construction is an **ensemble of adjacent *slow* lookbacks** (no fast leg), which
diversifies lookback specification-luck:

- **`slow2` {189,252} is the new held-out champion shape** — +0.606 vs +0.571, winning selection
  *and* held-out — a clean Sharpe-tilt upgrade. Recommend it as the live trend pole
  (`atalanta/trend_ensemble.yaml`); left `trend.yaml` untouched pending the user's call to repoint.
- **`slow3` {126,189,252} is the convexity-tilt sibling** — qskew +2.59 / crisis +0.169 /
  maxDD −16.8 on the real book, a touch less Sharpe. Per the roster's "decompose sub-roles",
  these are two **distinct Locked Candidates** spanning a Sharpe↔convexity frontier, both
  beating-or-matching the single champion on their respective axis.
- **On the trend pole's actual mandate** (convexity + drawdown; carry does income — the roster's
  division of labor), `slow3` is the better pole. On Sharpe, `slow2` is. Either dominates the
  single-252 champion; neither uses a fast leg.

### Theory anchors
CFM gamma identity / convexity-vs-Sharpe trade-off ([[convexity-as-the-axis-of-strategy-diversification]]):
smoothing/blending rebalances along the convexity frontier — slow3 buys convexity, slow2 buys
Sharpe. AQR "You Can't Always Trend": the drought is regime-intrinsic, so the win is *robustness*
(spec-luck diversification), not a recovered drought alpha. ReSolve/Etienne ensemble-robustness:
an equal-weighted blend beats the single-best spec out-of-sample — confirmed here.

### Limitations
- own-gamma t is statistically resolved only on the 120-mo US-proxy window; the 80-mo London
  window is underpowered for the monthly-efficiency regression (treat the London own-gamma as
  *unresolved*, not failed — the other four convexity signatures carry the membership there).
- score-blend (the shippable component) ≈ weight-blend (the §1–2 harness) on the **Tier-1** book;
  they diverge on the noisy US 2018-24 *slice* (qskew), so the component's authoritative numbers
  are the Tier-1 / US-long reads above, not the short slice.
- US proxy *understates* convexity (qskew ~+0.1 vs London +2.6); the long-window read is a
  drawdown/own-gamma stress test, not a convexity read — convexity is read on the real book.
- The drawdown improvement is **breadth-gated**: it holds at Tier-1 (20), inverts at Tier-0 (10).
  Do not shrink the universe below the ~20-name orthogonality plateau.

## Addendum — floor-objective re-test: the buffer is the keeper, the ensemble is NOT

The §1–4 verdict optimized the trend pole for **standalone Sharpe + spec-robustness**. Re-tested
against the pole's *actual* floor job — **crisis-window capture** (`/tmp/floor_pays_duration.py`'s
multi-window compounded crisis-median, carry_duration pole, real London Tier-1) — the ensemble
is the **wrong lever**, and a separate Sharpe lever (turnover buffering) is the right one.

**The trap: quarterly skew (shape) ≠ crisis-window capture (magnitude).** The slow ensemble
*raised* qskew (+2.36→+2.52) and smoothed daily whipsaw, but by reaching DOWN to 126/189-day legs
it fires later and **captures less of the crisis-window move**: trend-only crisis-median
**+7.86%→+3.50%** (slow3) and composite crisis **+5.56%→+3.16%**, with deeper drawdown.

**Speed sweep (`/tmp/trend_speed_crisis.py`, floor crisis-median):** shorter lookbacks make crisis
capture *worse*, not better — single 252 **+7.86%**, 189 +1.76, 126 −1.49, 63 +1.57, 21 −0.47.
On a *non-equity* book the crises (2018Q4/COVID/2022/2023) are **sustained macro trends** (bonds,
dollar) a slow signal is *already positioned in* before the shock; a fast signal whipsaws.
Going *slower* captures **more**: single 378 **+9.47%**. lb252 is simultaneously best Sharpe
(+0.69) and best drawdown (−17.0) — it Pareto-dominates on the floor's terms.

**The fixes that work (`/tmp/trend_crisis_fix.py` + `/tmp/trend_sharpe_sweep.py`):**

| floor trend pole | tSharpe | tCrisis% | compSh | compCrisis% | compMdd% |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| single 252 (orig champion) | +0.69 | +7.86 | +0.90 | +5.56 | −26.6 |
| **single 252 + buffer 0.20** | **+0.78** | **+8.76** | **+0.96** | **+6.18** | −26.2 |
| up-ens {252,315,378} + buffer 0.20 | +0.71 | +8.69 | +0.91 | +6.29 | −25.9 |
| slow2 {189,252} + buffer 0.20 (DOWN) | +0.78 | +6.05 | +0.96 | +4.56 | −26.7 |
| slow3 {126,189,252} (DOWN ensemble) | +0.56 | +3.50 | +0.84 | +3.16 | −27.9 |

- **Turnover buffering (no-trade band ≈0.20) is the real, validated win** — the one lever (of the
  literature KEEP set: vol-target/efficiency/sigmoid/breadth/cap all rejected on our data) that is
  a strict Pareto improvement: it lifts Sharpe (+0.69→+0.78), crisis (+7.86→+8.76), UPI and
  drawdown *together*, by removing micro-rebalancing whipsaw (Baltas-Kosowski: cut turnover >⅓
  without degradation — here *with* improvement). It is **speed-neutral**, so it keeps crisis
  capture. Component `atalanta.trendStraddleBuffered` (param `buffer_band`); held-out confirmed
  (buffered ensemble grid: best +0.689 vs ensemble +0.606 vs champion +0.571).
- **Ensembling DOWNWARD (slow2/slow3) is rejected for the floor pole** — it dilutes the crisis
  capture that is the pole's whole job; the buffer does not recover it (slow2+buf crisis +6.05 ≪
  single+buf +8.76). The bug was the *direction*: ensembling UPWARD {252,315,378} preserves crisis
  (+8.69) AND keeps spec-robustness, matching single+buffer on every floor metric at ~0.07 less Sharpe.

**Revised verdict (supersedes §-Verdict for the FLOOR pole):**
- **Floor trend pole = single lb252 + buffer 0.20** (simplest; lb252 is held-out-best among singles).
  Optionally **{252,315,378} + buffer 0.20** to diversify single-lookback spec-luck at ~0.07 Sharpe.
- **Buffer: KEEP** (universal Pareto win). **Downward slow-ensemble: DROP** for the floor.
- The slow ensemble retains a marginal *standalone* held-out-Sharpe edge — a potential non-floor
  (target-tier) Sharpe sleeve only; it is not the floor's drawdown-payer.
- Confirms roster line-77's framing: the trend pole's value here is **crisis capture**, and the
  floor's value is calm-market income/drawdown efficiency, not crisis outperformance.
