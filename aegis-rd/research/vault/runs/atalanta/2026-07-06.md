# 2026-07-06

## Goal

Second margin-priced leverage experiment (aegis-rd-wku4, GH #80): the LEVERED extension
pyramid - the literature's actual shape (Mulvaney tranche multiplier; Concretum VPP "add
units at profit thresholds, capped", best Profit Factor of the sizing family; CFM
"building into extended trends manufactures the right tail") - which the 2026-07-03
FORGO pyramid could not express under gross <= 1. Every trend leg enters at FULL
champion size (the FORGO kill mechanism - "the ramp's delay misses the convex front" -
is structurally absent) and ADDS financed exposure as the trade extends, m = 1 +
clip(r/pyr_k, 0, 1), pyr_cap pinned 2.0 (B1 ceiling, mandate). Funding priced
(margin_interest_rate at the tier-I default; IDTL borrow per ADR-0008).

### 20260706T190305253713Z_atalanta_trend_pyramid_levered

- **Hypothesis**: adding financed tranches to extended winners lifts held-out
  trend_convexity_payoff over the champion without breaching the floor sleeve's
  survivability envelope. New component atalanta.keystone_pyramid_levered v1.0.0
  (pyr_k=0 nested control, byte-identical champion); champion cell pinned
  (enter 0.10 / exit 0.05 / short_cap 0.10); pyr_k swept {0, 0.25, 0.5, 1.0}.
- **Keep/kill criteria** (pre-registered in aegis-rd-wku4 and the config header before
  launching): H1 best active cell beats the in-grid control on held-out convexity with
  a non-collapsing profile; H2 worst-split max_dd <= 1.5x control's; H3 return-vs-gross
  degradation consistent with the priced rate. KILL on monotone convexity destruction
  or H2 breach at every active pyr_k; no tolerance-tuning.
- **What changed since previous run**: vs the killed FORGO pyramid - the ramp direction
  (m in [1, 2] adding to winners, instead of [0, 1] holding back entries); leverage
  financed and priced instead of forbidden.
- **Result** (held-out means; control = pyr_k 0): convexity 0.9658 / 0.8955 / 0.8249 at
  pyr_k 0.25 / 0.5 / 1.0 vs control 0.4451 - every levered cell beats the champion,
  the control ranks LAST of the grid, smooth decay profile (no spec-luck). Returns
  +25.2 / +23.7 / +21.4 vs +14.5% per half. BUT worst-split max_dd 19.78 / 19.53 /
  18.77% vs control 10.18% = 1.94x / 1.92x / 1.84x - ALL above the 1.5x bar. Sharpe
  1.02 / 0.96 / 0.90 vs 1.15; fees ~2.2-2.4x. dd scales ~with the added gross (~2x at
  extension peaks -> ~1.9x dd), so H3 passes - no unexplained bleed, the funding and
  give-back arithmetic are exactly what the priced sim predicts. The pyr_k=1.0 cell was
  not persisted by role selection (best/median/worst of 4); probed with a one-cell
  pinned run (trend_pyramid_levered_k1), then the probe config removed.
- **Verdict**: kill (H2 fail at every active cell; H1 and H3 passed). The literature
  shape WORKS - convexity doubles, the FORGO diagnosis is confirmed in reverse - but
  the dose breaches the floor mandate: this sleeve is the tier the book leans on DURING
  dislocations, and a 1.9x worst-split drawdown is the wrong tail to fatten for a
  drawdown-payer. Kill per pre-registration, no tuning. Champion (lock
  20260629T000858802034Z) stands.

### atalanta_cs_equity_momentum (v1) - the convex non-trend complement

- **Hypothesis** (GH #80 trend-Q1 hyp #2, [[where-convexity-still-lives]]): a dollar-neutral
  CROSS-SECTIONAL equity-momentum leg is convex via dispersion and near-orthogonal to the
  champion, so it is a candidate to COMBINE with the keystone champion and lift the combined
  held-out trend_convexity_payoff at |corr| < 0.10. This run measures the STANDALONE leg first;
  combination is only worth building if the leg is convex AND low-corr AND fee-survivable.
- **New component**: atalanta.cross_sectional_momentum v1.0.0 - ranks the cross-section by
  trend_score each bar, longs top n_side / shorts bottom n_side, inverse-vol within each side,
  each side normalized to 0.5 gross (book gross 1 / net 0, exact dollar-neutral).
- **Basket** (IBKR-gateway-locked, UCITS ETF, USD/LSEETF): CSPX, CJPU, IEMU, CCAU, IDBZ. The
  EM USD lines (CSKR ~2020, CMXC ~2021) are too young for the 2018 window - a GBP-primary-line
  v2 is the path to a fuller EM basket. lookback 126, vol_window 126, n_side 2, band 0.15,
  fixed_fee 1.25 on init_cash 5000.
- **Keep/kill criteria** (pre-registered BEFORE results): (1) FEE GATE first - if the fill count
  makes fees dominate (net return materially below gross, per the destsweep failure mode) the leg
  is dead on the EUR 5k book regardless of shape; (2) STANDALONE CONVEXITY - held-out
  trend_convexity_payoff > 0 (the leg is genuinely convex on its own); (3) ORTHOGONALITY -
  |monthly corr to the champion stream| < 0.10. A leg that clears all three earns a combination
  test; failing (1) kills at the book size, failing (2) kills the mechanism, failing (3) means it
  is not the diversifier the hypothesis needs. Robustness follow-up (v28 guard): re-read across
  rank lookbacks 63/126/252 before any promotion. No tolerance-tuning.
- **Result** (run `20260706T212637000700Z_atalanta_cs_equity_momentum`, held-out, 5 splits, single
  cell n_side=2): trend_convexity_payoff by split -0.112 / -0.335 / +0.015 / -0.146 / +0.177, **mean
  -0.080** (NEGATIVE, 4/5 splits <= 0). total_return -7.2 / -16.0 / -1.2 / -6.9 / +7.4, mean
  **-4.8%/half**; Sharpe mean **-0.53**; fees ~72 EUR/split (~0.7-1%/yr on 5k) at ~18 trades/split;
  market_beta mean **-0.03** (dollar-neutral achieved). Selection-set quarterly skew -0.54 vs
  held-out +0.26 = unstable small-sample, the [[skewness-in-asset-returns]] caveat.
- **Verdict**: **KILL** (pre-registered). Convexity criterion FAIL (held-out payoff negative, the
  opposite of the hired shape); fee gate FAIL (fees compound a loss-making leg on the 5k book);
  orthogonality PASS (market_beta ~0 - the dollar-neutral construction genuinely strips net equity
  beta, so the *machinery* is sound). The mechanism did not transfer: cross-sectional momentum on a
  THIN, DM-heavy 5-name UCITS equity basket is non-convex and loss-making - the v28 thin-universe
  fragility reproduced. Versor's convex GET result rests on 24 markets (12 DM + 12 EM); the
  dispersion that manufactures crisis convexity needs EM breadth we cannot reach because the EM USD
  lines are too young (CSKR ~2020, CMXC ~2021). The same BREADTH CEILING that caps the trend sleeve
  ([[is-the-trend-premium-decaying]]) caps this complement. Re-entry: ONLY a GBP-primary-line v2
  with a genuinely broad DM+EM basket (>=10-12 markets, EUR/GBP FX) tests the mechanism fairly; even
  then the EUR 5k fee floor is a structural headwind, so the honest prior is that it fails on fees
  before convexity. Champion (lock 20260629T000858802034Z) stands; no combination test earned.

## Day summary

Two threads, both clean pre-registration kills. (1) The LEVERED extension pyramid: the
second first-class use of the margin-interest machinery, opposite outcome to the demeter
unclip - there funding ate a thin carry; here the mechanism SUCCEEDED on its own metric
(financed pyramiding manufactures convexity, +117% held-out at the fast cell) and died on
the survivability gate instead. (2) The convex NON-TREND complement (GH #80 hyp #2): built
a new cross-sectional-momentum component and a gateway-locked country-equity basket; the
dollar-neutral machinery works (market_beta ~0) but the leg is non-convex and loss-making
on a thin DM-heavy 5-name UCITS basket - the mechanism needs a DM+EM breadth the young EM
USD lines block, the trend sleeve's breadth ceiling reappearing on the complement. The
champion stands after both.
