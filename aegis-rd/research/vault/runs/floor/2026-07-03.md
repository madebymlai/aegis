# 2026-07-03

## Goal

Re-verify the floor blend ([[runs/floor/2026-06-14]]) with BOTH poles upgraded since:
trend = the 3-name non-equity keystone_shortmute champion (`atalanta/trend.yaml`, locked
2026-06-29); carry = the SOTA pole promoted today (`demeter/carry_sota.yaml`, SDHY+LQDH+
15% IEML under live band/fee mechanics, locked 20260703T183523549479Z). The 2026-06-14
composite numbers (UPI 0.91 -> 2.17, w*=0.60, fixed 0.40 rule) all cite the OLD poles.

## Runs

### floor re-verification, upgraded poles (prototype diagnostic)

Harness `scratchpad/floor_blend_verify.py` (agent-built, real pipeline: `load_run_config`
-> market data (warm cache, healthy, real EUR conversion) -> indicator + strategy
components at the locked cells -> `simulate_single_book`). Full window 2018-01..2024-12,
1767 aligned obs, zero calendar drops. Fixed-weight blend in vol-normalized risk space
per the 2026-06-14 addendum (formula is a reconstruction of the diary's prose - flagged).
SPY benchmark; crisis metric = the unmodified `_crisis_quarter_return`.

- **Hypothesis**: the upgraded carry pole preserves (or improves) the floor's income-
  overlay value - composite UPI at the fixed ~0.40 carry weight at least matches the old
  pole's lift, without deepening composite drawdown vs trend-only.
- **Keep/kill criteria** (this is a verification diagnostic; the bar is the 2026-06-14
  division of labor): composite at w~0.40 should lift UPI vs trend-only at tolerable
  crisis dilution; and the pole must remain a genuine concave anchor (qskew clearly
  negative, corr(trend) ~<= 0).
- **What changed since previous run**: both poles. Trend: 20-name London book -> 3-name
  IDTL/IGLN/ICOM keystone_shortmute (a much stronger standalone: Sharpe 1.11, UPI 2.93,
  qskew +0.95, crisis +20.7%, maxDD -10.75%). Carry: IHYU+LQDH unbanded -> SDHY+LQDH+15%
  IEML banded.
- **Result**: the floor does NOT pay on this window, at any weight - and the new pole is
  a *worse floor partner* despite being a better standalone sleeve:

  | | trend-only | w=0.30 | w=0.40 | w=0.50 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Sharpe | **1.11** | 1.05 | 0.98 | 0.88 |
  | UPI | **2.93** | 2.80 | 2.43 | 1.96 |
  | maxDD | **-10.75%** | -12.23% | -12.71% | -13.07% |
  | crisis | **+20.73%** | +9.76% | +6.90% | +4.38% |
  | qskew | **+0.95** | +0.60 | +0.56 | +0.51 |

  Standalone carry: Sharpe 0.24, UPI 0.29, maxDD -15.0%, qskew **-0.49**, crisis -3.74%.
  **corr(trend, carry) = +0.173** - the old pole was -0.11 to -0.13. Two causes stack:
  (1) the pole-level optimization (income utility + tail budget, banded mechanics)
  traded away exactly the properties the floor hired it for - the deep concave anchor
  (qskew -1.83 -> -0.49, now *borderline* on the <= -0.5 family gate) and the negative
  correlation to trend; (2) the 3-name trend champion is so strong standalone (UPI 2.93
  vs the old 0.91) that the income overlay has nothing left to lift - the 06-14 UPI
  story (0.91 -> 2.17) existed because the old trend pole had a weak calm-market path.
  Vol-normalizing the blend makes it worse, not better (it upweights the low-vol,
  now-positively-correlated carry stream); even the raw blend at w=0.40 (Sharpe 1.08,
  UPI 2.91, maxDD -11.05%) at best *matches* trend-only while diluting crisis capture
  by half.
- **Verdict**: **iterate** - two distinct lessons, neither killing "Floor pays" as a
  mechanism: (1) **pole promotion needs a composite-level gate**: today's sweeps ranked
  the pole standalone (utility + tail), and the winner quietly shed its portfolio role;
  any future pole champion must also clear qskew <= -0.5 (hard, not borderline) and a
  corr-to-trend ceiling (~<= 0) measured on the aligned window. (2) **the floor pairing
  is conditional on the trend pole's regime**: against a trend sleeve this dominant on
  its own selection window, no carry weight adds value; the floor's case must be argued
  on trend-weak regimes (2015-2019-style trend winters) that this 2018-2025 window
  barely contains - or wait for the live trend pole to meet one.

## Day summary

The upgraded carry pole is a better *sleeve* and a worse *floor partner*: standalone
income-per-tail improved all day, but the composite check shows the improvements came
partly out of the pole's concave anchor (qskew -1.83 -> -0.49, corr to trend flipping
positive). With the 3-name trend champion this strong, trend-only dominates every floor
weight on every metric on this window. The floor thesis survives as a mechanism (the
division of labor is intact) but its measured value is currently zero against this trend
pole - and the day's deepest lesson is procedural: a pole optimized standalone can
silently defect from its portfolio role, so promotion gates must include the composite.
