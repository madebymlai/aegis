The levered measured drag is -3.91pp/yr CAGR and -0.39 Sharpe versus the
zero-rate control. That lands above the pre-registered 2.0-2.6pp/yr EUR-rate
band, so I stopped and reconciled the currency split before updating records.
The charge is entirely USD in this book:

- Levered: USD 352,177.9471 at 5.13%, 2554 elapsed days, ACT/360 implies
  average net USD debit of about USD 967,668.
- Gross-1.0: USD 154,444.0142 implies average net USD debit of about USD
  424,361.

Verdict: not a netting bug. The old analytic band assumed EUR-rate-dominant
funding on a single blended loan. Nautilus/IBKR-style per-currency accounting
funds the USD-quoted UCITS legs with USD debits; positive EUR cash does not
offset USD loans. The gross-1.0 baseline therefore also moved more than bps
because it still holds USD-denominated legs funded by a USD cash debit. That is
an accepted deviation from the original gross-1.0 bps expectation: the bps
expectation shared the same EUR-rate-dominant funding assumption the
band-sensitivity note superseded.

## Futures charge-base correction

The superseding `aegis-rd-wft1` note was handled after review: research margin
interest now charges broker-visible cash, not raw VBT cash, by adding each
futures-masked column's signed marked position value back to group cash before
calling `debit_interest`. The mask is derived from `data.futures` bare roots and
is all-False for current champion configs.

Additional fixtures passed:

- levered pure-futures book: zero margin-interest charge;
- mixed spot+futures book: charge only on the spot-financed cash deficit;
- all-False futures mask: byte-identical value series versus the mask-free run.

Documented residual: locked initial margin is not modeled as consumed cash, so a
levered mixed futures/spot research book undercharges by rate x margin
requirement. This is second-order relative to the full-notional double-charge
the mask removes.
