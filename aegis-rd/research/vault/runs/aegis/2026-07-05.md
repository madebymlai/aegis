# 2026-07-05

## Goal

Calibrate the live book's `[tail_convexity_budget]` candidate values from measurement
instead of operator guesses - the deferred item from [[2026-07-04]] ("recalibrate against
these anchors when the risk-share -> notional scaling is pinned"). Method: `aegis-trader
backtest` of the real commingled book (book.toml, all three sleeves, 1M EUR, IBKR fee
model) over 2018-01-01..2024-12-31 (the widest window every leg covers in the catalog;
LVO alone reaches 2013), at five tail-budget sizes: risk share 0 (tail off), 0.0065,
0.0125, 0.025, 0.05. Not an aerd run - a trader-side calibration study like p3; no
candidate store artifacts, scripts and CSVs in the session scratchpad
(`run_variant.py`, `calibrate.py`, `decompose.py`).

## Runs

### tail-budget size sweep (trader backtest, 2018-2024)

- **Hypothesis**: realized LVO notional scales ~linearly with declared risk share, so
  per-unit-risk-share payoff/carry candidate values exist and generalize.
- **Method note**: the book-level (variant minus tail-off) return difference decomposes
  into the sleeve's OWN contribution (w_lvo(t-1) x r_lvo(t), from the fills ledger) plus
  an allocator displacement residual. Only sleeve-own is calibratable: displacement is
  +/-1pp/yr and flips sign between adjacent sizes (rebalance-timing chaos) in calm years.
- **Result** (sleeve-own, per variant):

| risk share | calm mean w | crash own (Feb19-Mar31 2020) | calm-year own drag |
| ---------- | ----------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------ |
| 0.05       | 2.5%        | +2.00pp                      | -1.59pp/yr         |
| 0.025      | 2.2%        | +1.93pp                      | -1.19pp/yr         |
| 0.0125     | 2.2%        | +1.92pp                      | -1.16pp/yr         |
| 0.0065     | 2.2%        | +1.87pp                      | -1.00pp/yr         |

  Calm years = 2019/2021/2023/2024 (LVO bled 29-53%/yr in each - harsher than the
  -23%/yr 13-year average the article carries). 2022 grind: own ~-1.0pp. 2018: ~flat
  positive. Per unit of NOTIONAL (the size-invariant form): crash delivery +2.9 to
  +3.7, calm bleed -0.45 to -0.63/yr.
- **Verdict**: **hypothesis dead, better one measured.** The hierarchical-ERC allocator
  holds the lone negatively-correlated Target-group sleeve at its minimum-hedge weight:
  realized notional is FLAT in risk share across an 8x dial range. The budget dial is
  effectively on/off; the menu while ON is one package: ~2.2% notional calm, ~+1.9% NAV
  delivered over the 2020 crash window, ~1.0-1.6% NAV/yr cost in vol-crush years.

### findings worth keeping

1. **Vol-targeting shrinks the hedge exactly when it matters**: the book entered the
   COVID crash at only ~0.6% LVO notional (LVO vol was already elevated); the +1.9pp
   delivery came from the ~3x spike, not from size. A milder crash pays proportionally
   less - `crisis_reliability = 0.75` stays operator-judged, n=1.
2. **Drawdown-governor bonus**: in 2020 the hedged book beat the tail-off book by
   +4.9 to +5.2pp at book level - only ~+1.9pp is sleeve-own; the rest is the governor
   NOT delevering the hedged book into the drawdown. Real, but path-specific; kept out
   of the budget arithmetic (noted in book.toml comments).
3. **The former mandate (1% coverage for <= 0.75%/yr) is unbuyable** - no smaller
   package exists on the dial. Declared-vs-real bookkeeping was off ~2x in both
   directions of the old operator guesses (payoff 0.20 -> measured 0.76/unit rs at the
   landing point; carry 0.08 -> measured 0.48).

## Decision

book.toml recalibrated to the self-consistent measured point at risk share 0.025
(landing point of the fill): `coverage_target 0.019`, `annual_carry_budget 0.012`,
candidate `payoff_at_attachment 0.76`, `annual_carry 0.48`, reliability/capacity
unchanged. Declared arithmetic now reproduces measurement exactly (fill lands at
rs=0.025; declared delivery 1.9%, declared carry 1.2%/yr). The realized book at that
point is MEASURED (the rs=0.025 variant), not extrapolated; final verification run with
the edited book.toml matches. Candidate values are valid ONLY for rs in [0.0065, 0.05] -
per-rs values scale ~1/rs because the notional is flat; never extrapolate linearly.

Flagged for the operator: the mandate knobs changed meaning (0.01/0.0075 -> 0.019/0.012)
because the old pair described a package the allocator cannot buy; the traded book is
unchanged within noise (rs 0.0234 -> 0.025, identical realized weights).

## B1: the leverage decision (executed)

The under-levered finding was measured and closed the same day. Bundle caps raised to
gross/net 2.0 in all three champion configs (ceilings only - sleeve weights unchanged),
wheels re-exported and force-reinstalled; book variant at max_book_gross 1.75 backtested
2018-2024: vol 5.0% -> 8.4% (93% of the 9% target), Sharpe 1.21 -> **1.74**
gross-of-funding, return +6.2% -> +15.3%/yr, maxDD -7.5% -> -10.2%, crash month -1.5% ->
-4.4%. The Sharpe RISE is the finding: the gross cap had been clipping exposure hardest
in calm (low-vol) regimes - exactly carry's best years - so unclipping adds a
volatility-managed timing gain on top of scale; fixed fees also amortize better on
larger fills. Funding is NOT engine-modeled: ~67% NAV borrowed; the full IBKR EUR loan
rate (ESTR ~1.9% + tier spread 1.5% below 100k / 1.0% to 1M ~= 3.0-3.4%) charges the
RETURN ~ -2.0 to -2.6%/yr of NAV -> **~+13%/yr net**; Sharpe loses only the spread
(excess-return basis) -> **~1.6 net**. Operator decision: keep 1.75
(book.toml updated; maxDD floor re-baselined). Caveats on record: single window, the
calm-leverage timing gain may be regime-generous, funding overlay is analytic - a
margin-interest model in the engine is the follow-up before incubation.

## Day summary

Risk-share -> notional scaling pinned by direct measurement: there is no scaling - the
ERC allocator's minimum-hedge weight makes the tail sleeve size structural (~2.2% calm),
and the convexity budget picks the declared point, not the size. book.toml now carries
measured, self-consistent budget numbers with their validity range documented in-file.
