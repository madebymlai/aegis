# 2026-06-14

## Goal

First aegis session under the **UCITS / EU-retail / no-account-derivatives** constraint.
The old aegis family (US-ETF universe: SPY/.../UUP, VIXY, TAIL, BTAL) is non-tradable for
an EU-retail book (PRIIPs blocks US ETFs), so the target-tier tail sleeve is rebuilt from
the menu in [[the-ucits-constrained-tail-sleeve]]. This session pre-registers a
two-candidate bake-off against the **atalanta UCITS trend floor**
(`research/configs/atalanta/trend.yaml`, `trendStraddleBuffered` on the 20-name orthogonal
non-equity book): a Regime-A rented-vol tail (`VOOL`) versus a Regime-B physical haven
anchor (CHF + gold). `SPQH` is dropped - a capped buffer (-3% to -12%) with no crisis
history and a single swap counterparty, dominated as a tail by `VOOL` and as a defensive
anchor by the physical basket.

This is **pre-registration only** - the gating UCITS aegis tail component does not yet
exist (see Next). Gates below are fixed before any code is written or run launched.

## Numeraire

**EUR base.** `VOOL` and the FX legs are native-EUR, so their crisis payoff is in the
numeraire we spend. Flag: the pipeline is currently **currency-naive** (no FX conversion -
verified, no fx/numeraire code in `research/aegis_research/`), so USD-quoted legs (`SGLN`,
`IBC1`) must be converted to EUR or their dollar/gold convexity is mis-measured. Resolve
before v2/v3 (see Next).

## Runs (gates pre-registered before launch; all `(pending launch)` until the component ships)

### (pending launch) ucits_aegis_v1_voolfloor

- **Hypothesis**: a small fixed-weight `VOOL` floor - final book = (1 - w)*atalanta-trend
  + w*`VOOL`, continuously rebalanced (the weight is the monetization rule) - improves the
  held-out fast-crash splits (2018, 2020) over the trend-only control. From
  [[the-ucits-constrained-tail-sleeve]] h1. **Pre-registered prior**: most likely FAILS as
  VIXY did (the VIX roll-bleed is the variance risk premium, not carryable at any weight
  that moves the tail); a pass is the first genuine immediate-gamma tail the UCITS box admits.
- **Keep/kill criteria** (pre-registered): sweep w in {0, 0.02, 0.05}. KEEP if some w>0
  improves the 2018 split Sharpe by >= +0.3 over the in-run w=0 control AND composite >=
  control - 0.05 AND neither calm split (2019, 2021, 2023) drops > 0.3. KILL if no w clears
  the 2018 gate, or every improvement costs more composite than it adds. Decision output is
  the head-to-head vs the physical anchor (v2), not just the gate.
- **What changed**: new UCITS aegis tail component (fixed-weight hedge floor over the
  atalanta trend book); universe = atalanta 20-name + `VOOL` as the held hedge symbol;
  window 2013-01 to 2025-01 (VOOL inception binds start), EUR, from_rolling splits matched
  to atalanta (length 504, split 0.5, max_splits 10).

### (pending launch) ucits_aegis_v2_physical_anchor

- **Hypothesis**: an always-on CHF + gold basket (`EURCHF` + `SGLN`, fixed weights,
  rebalanced) added to the trend floor clears the *defensive* gates but **not** the
  *strongly*-tail magnitudes, and improves the floor's transition-window drawdowns at lower
  calm-year bleed than `VOOL`. From h2.
- **Keep/kill criteria** (pre-registered): sweep gold/CHF split and total weight. KEEP as a
  defensive anchor if the basket improves the floor's worst transition-split drawdown AND
  composite >= control - 0.05 AND crisis-conditional return >= 0. Record whether
  bear-regime beta clears the "strongly negative" tail bar (expected: it does not - a
  tail-grade FAIL here is not a kill, it confirms "anchor, not tail").

### (pending launch) ucits_aegis_v3_dollar_leg

- **Hypothesis** (h3): adding a small short USD leg (`IBC1`/`EURUSD`) to the CHF+gold basket
  helps the growth-shock cell (2020) but *hurts* 2022 and 2025-style rate/tariff shocks -
  testing whether the structural smirk has demoted the dollar from anchor to optional.
- **Keep/kill criteria** (pre-registered): KEEP the USD leg only if it improves
  min(2020, 2022) split return over the no-USD basket. The smirk reading is SUPPORTED if it
  helps 2020 but degrades 2022; REFUTED if the dollar still lifts both (anchor intact).

### (pending launch) ucits_aegis_v4_bakeoff

- **Hypothesis** (h4, the roster's open *Target earns its bleed*): rank floor+`VOOL` against
  floor+physical-basket by held-out Calmar and crisis-conditional return; the winner "earns
  its bleed". Decides whether the UCITS tail tier is a rented-vol tail, a physical anchor,
  or a small blend.
- **Keep/kill criteria** (pre-registered): the leg with higher held-out Calmar AND
  crisis-conditional return at matched volatility is promoted; a blend is promoted only if
  it dominates both singles on the convexity-per-bleed frontier (Calmar AND crisis-cond >=
  the better single).
