# 2026-06-14

## Goal

First aegis session under the **UCITS / EU-retail / no-account-derivatives** constraint.
The old aegis family (US-ETF universe: SPY/.../UUP, VIXY, TAIL, BTAL) is non-tradable for
an EU-retail book (PRIIPs blocks US ETFs), so the target-tier tail sleeve is rebuilt from
the menu in [[the-ucits-constrained-tail-sleeve]]. This session pre-registers a
two-candidate bake-off against the **atalanta UCITS trend floor**
(`research/configs/atalanta/trend.yaml`, `trendStraddleBuffered` on the 20-name orthogonal
non-equity book): a Regime-A rented-vol tail (`VOOL`) versus a Regime-B physical haven
anchor (CHF + gold). `SPQH` is dropped - a capped buffer (-3% to -12%) with no crisis
history and a single swap counterparty, dominated as a tail by `VOOL` and as a defensive
anchor by the physical basket.

This is **pre-registration only** - the gating UCITS aegis tail component does not yet
exist (see Next). Gates below are fixed before any code is written or run launched.

## Architecture correction (later 2026-06-14): build the strategy standalone, defer the blend to the allocator

The pre-registered v1-v4 above were framed as a tail *overlaid on the atalanta floor* (one
composite run) plus a Regime-A-vs-B bake-off. Two corrections this session:

- **Strategies are built standalone; combining them is the allocator's job.** atalanta (trend)
  and demeter (carry) are each their own strategy, scored on their own stream; the target-tier
  tail is the same - its own strategy. The fund-of-funds layer that overlays the tail on the
  floor (the roster itself) is the **allocator**, which **does not exist yet**. So aegis carries
  no atalanta logic, and the *relative* "Target earns its bleed" gate (vs trend-only) is the
  allocator's, not this run's. v1's pre-registered relative gate is therefore **deferred**, not run.
- **Regime A only, accept the verdict.** The mandate ruling is Regime A (a UCITS fund may hold
  derivatives internally; a `VOOL` unit is a transferable security, not an account derivative) -
  the only regime that reaches a genuine immediate-gamma tail. The Regime-B physical basket
  (v2/v3) and the A-vs-B bake-off (v4) are dropped.

New component shipped: **`aegis.heldHedge`** (`research/components/strategies/aegis/aegis_held_hedge.py`):
a fixed-weight, inverse-vol-shaped, continuously-rebalanced hold of the tail leg(s) at full fixed
exposure - **not** vol-targeted, because a tail must be present precisely when its own vol spikes;
trailing `n_benchmark` legs are carried benchmark-only (excluded from the book via NaN). Holding the
fixed weight *is* the monetization rule. Config: `research/configs/aegis/tail_vool.yaml`.
