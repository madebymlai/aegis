# Finding a Buildable Convergent Engine

> [!abstract] Active decision
> There is no demonstrated Demeter winner to promote now. After restarting the search across academic evidence, practitioner construction and alternative convergence mechanisms, the strongest first prototype is a **whole-share book of definitive fixed-cash merger targets**, sized by break-loss contribution and allowed to hold cash. It ranks public deal-events whose contractual offer supplies the anchor; it does not rank generic stocks or merely buy the widest spread. A broad-stock challenger remains useful, but the evidence does not support compressing residual reversal to six names: test monthly industry/factor-relative residual reversal at `12/20/30/40` total holdings against a `20 long / 20 short` benchmark. Fractions do not solve this because IBKR's US fractional commission is approximately 1% per side. A practitioner-style value/quality portfolio is a valid sparse-alpha comparator, but it overlaps the off-axis alpha job more than the convergence seat. The first working hypothesis is therefore **anchor first, residual second**: qualify contractual events first, then test whether news-adjusted residual information improves entry without increasing turnover or break concentration.

## The question

The job defined by [[the-tiered-strategy-roster]] is an **ordinary-market return engine** that can help finance the persistent-crisis responder. A candidate must therefore have:

1. a named payer and an economic or contractual convergence anchor;
2. a positive full-cycle return after the costs of the actual implementation;
3. a legible failure state;
4. positive marginal whole-book utility beside Atalanta;
5. a causal, live-updatable data contract;
6. enough internal or external breadth that one idiosyncratic event is not the sleeve.

Negative skew is neither necessary nor sufficient. It may describe merger arbitrage, credit, FX carry or option writing, but it does not define the job. A symmetric diversified carry factor can qualify; a negatively skewed yield wrapper with no net premium cannot.

This note asks which mechanisms survive the actual portfolio constraints: an approximately EUR 5,000 commingled book, ordinary IBKR retail access, UCITS/EEA eligibility where packaged products are used, fixed per-order costs, public or affordable causal data, and paired evaluation with the locked trend engine. A broad signal universe is allowed. The implementation problem is to preserve its information while minimizing the number and cost of trades.

## What won in the literature, and why it does not compress

The institutional winner remains **diversified long/short alternative risk premia**. Koijen et al. find carry across nine applications and report a diversified Sharpe ratio near `1.1` after their estimated costs. Their construction averages signals to reduce turnover, ranks cross-sectionally and spreads risk across equities, bonds, rates, commodities and currencies.[^koijen] AQR's implementable expression combines value, momentum, carry and defensive long/short portfolios across the same broad opportunity set.[^aqrarp]

The breadth is load-bearing. The AQR fund's published exposure runs through hundreds of long and short positions and derivatives across four asset groups; it is not a two-fund rotation with a sophisticated name. A currently visible EUR retail share class also reports a minimum around EUR 9,100, already above the present book, and the minimum must be rechecked with the actual broker before relying on it.[^aqrminimum] Even if access improves, the multi-style fund spans Demeter, expansion and part of Atalanta's momentum job, so it would need exposure attribution rather than automatic assignment to one seat.

This separates three answers that should not be collapsed:

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Strongest generic evidence | Diversified long/short carry and multi-style alternative risk premia |
| Best clean convergence mechanism | Announced-deal merger arbitrage |
| Best path to proprietary small-book alpha | Cost-aware broad-universe residual reversal, subject to the active data and execution proof |
| Correct production allocation today | Empty seat or cash until an implementation passes |

There is no evidence-backed small, sparse strategy that wins all four rows.

## Decision after the restarted broad-to-narrow search

Three independent searches produced different winners because they answered different questions:

| Question | Winner | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cleanest sparse **convergence mechanism** | Definitive fixed-cash merger arbitrage | The signed agreement and fixed offer provide a contractual anchor; only the target stock is needed |
| Best-supported modern **reversal signal** | Monthly industry/factor-relative residual reversal with a wide buy/hold spread | It removes systematic exposures that contaminate raw reversal and trades more slowly than the weekly version |
| Strongest practitioner **sparse equity construction** | Industry-relative value conditioned on quality | Its signal decays slowly enough for patient execution, integer projection and rationed turnover |

The portfolio role breaks the tie. A generic value/quality long-short book belongs naturally beside other cross-sectional factor alpha in the off-axis seat. It may be an excellent strategy, but renaming it convergence would not create a distinct economic job. Merger arbitrage has a named payer, a dated resolution path and a legible failure payoff, so it is the first Demeter research build. Residual reversal remains the most relevant challenger because it supplies liquidity and directly tests statistical convergence.

The evidence also rejects one earlier shortcut: a broad rank does not make a sparse holding set broad. Published residual-reversal tests on the largest 100 US stocks use quintiles, which means approximately `20 long + 20 short`; live institutional market-neutral portfolios commonly use far more names.[^residualreversal] Pairs trading can use five pairs, but cost-aware evidence finds both simple pairs and industry reversal largely unprofitable after 2002.[^post2002pairs] A recent sparse selector averaged about six names when invested, but was in cash during 284 of 612 out-of-sample months, flattened after 2005 and omitted trading costs.[^sparsealpha] Six to twelve positions are therefore a **compression hypothesis**, not an evidence-backed minimum.

### The first prototype: a cash-deal event inventory

Rank pending deal-events, not arbitrary stocks. Admit only deals whose point-in-time primary documents establish a signed definitive agreement and fixed cash consideration. Initially exclude stock or mixed consideration, collars, contingent value rights, earnouts and ambiguous exchange ratios. EDGAR supplies free real-time submissions metadata and historical filing archives, so every term and status change can retain its source timestamp.[^edgarapi]

For event `i`, record:

- executable target price `P_i`;
- fixed offer `O_i`;
- conservative stand-alone break value `B_i`;
- independently estimated completion probability `q_i`;
- conservative time-to-close `T_i`;
- commissions, spread, FX and operational cost `C_i`.

The minimal decision quantity is:

$$
EV_i = q_i(O_i-P_i) - (1-q_i)(P_i-B_i) - C_i.
$$

Annualized spread is a report metric, not the ranker: a large near-deadline spread can be evidence of failure rather than richness. Begin with transparent base rates and deal facts, not a flexible completion model. Size positions by loss if the deal breaks, cap each event's break-loss contribution, and hold cash when fewer than roughly ten independent clean deals clear the hurdle. Mitchell and Pulvino's implementable construction likewise caps individual deals, includes costs and shows sharply worse market sensitivity in severe declines; the premium is compensation for event risk, not a free bond substitute.[^mitchell]

> [!warning] Novel inference: anchor first, residual second
> After the event-only benchmark exists, test whether announcement- and news-adjusted residual weakness improves entry among already qualified deals. This is not yet a validated published strategy. Subsequent legal, regulatory or financing news can look like a liquidity residual while actually changing completion probability, so every known deal-news window must be excluded and the overlay must improve net utility without increasing break concentration.

### The stock challenger: broad target, sparse projection

For the standalone challenger, rank liquid large-cap common stocks, not ETFs. ETFs can estimate or hedge sector and factor exposure, but an ETF-only cross-section discards the firm-specific dispersion that drives residual reversal. Use monthly standardized return residuals relative to industry and common factors, reverse the sign, enter only the tails and retain positions through a much wider hold band. The published practitioner shorthand is `buy 10 / hold 50`; it is a mechanism to test, not a parameter to copy blindly.[^shortsignals]

Construct the continuous neutral target first, then jointly solve whole-share membership and quantities. GMO's live mixed-integer construction is the relevant precedent: jointly controlling names, tickets, expected return, sector exposure, liquidity and cost cut names and tickets substantially without deleting the target's main exposures.[^gmo] The executed research frontier is `12/20/30/40` total positions, compared with both the continuous target and a 40-stock benchmark. Report target-to-executed weight correlation, signal capture, tracking error, realized beta/industry residuals, tickets, costs and concentration. Reject the sleeve if apparent standalone performance improves while transfer from the broad target collapses.

Membership hysteresis and allocator drift bands solve different problems. Membership hysteresis controls which stocks Demeter replaces while its sleeve weight is unchanged; allocator drift bands control transfers between sleeves. The strategy still needs the former even though Aegis already owns the latter.

### Evidence-derived patterns worth testing

These are research hypotheses, not established alphas:

1. **Break-loss inventory:** treat each clean cash deal as an item with expected value, resolution time and failure loss; select events by portfolio break-loss budget rather than spread or notional.
2. **Reservation alpha:** an incumbent keeps its position unless a challenger improves expected holding-period value by more than the complete switch cost plus an uncertainty reserve. This adapts practitioner `priority-best` turnover rationing to fixed retail tickets.[^smartrebalancing]
3. **Shadow-target transfer:** retain the continuous broad stock target even when it cannot be traded. Attribute lost signal separately to whole-share granularity, borrow, concentration and ticket limits instead of declaring the sparse book equivalent.
4. **Industry-pair auction:** for the stock challenger, generate feasible whole-share long/short pairs within industries, then select non-overlapping pairs jointly. This uses each scarce ticket to preserve local neutrality, but must still be rejected if 12 names do not retain the broad target.
5. **Opportunity-adaptive cash:** neither the event book nor the stock challenger is forced to be fully invested. Cash is a valid output when no event clears conservative expected value or no sparse projection preserves the signal.

## Stateful checkpoint: breadth is a construction problem

The earlier wrapper-only conclusion was too restrictive, but IBKR's interfaces must not be conflated. IBKR advertises fractional access to eligible US, Canadian and European stocks and ETFs in its interactive platforms, while margin accounts support stock shorting subject to locates and borrow costs.[^ibkrfractional][^ibkrshort] Its TWS socket API and its Web API do not have the same fractional-order contract.

Aegis already represents fractional quantities natively. Live sizing reads the venue instrument's `size_increment`, rounds each target delta to that increment and passes the resulting quantity to Nautilus. The current execution client is Nautilus's TWS/IB Gateway adapter. A paper-Gateway what-if probe on 2026-07-14 rejected a `0.1`-share AAPL sell with API error `10243`, while accepting a one-share AAPL sell as `PreSubmitted` with no order error. No order was transmitted. This proves a limitation of the current transport, not of Aegis's domain model or every IBKR interface.

IBKR added stock `cashQty` purchases to its Web API on 2026-03-27.[^ibkrwebchangelog] Its current OpenAPI contract defines order `quantity` as a number for either `BUY` or `SELL`, exposes account-level `supportsFractions` and `supportsCashQty` flags, and exposes contract-specific fractional order types and decimal precision through `fraqTypes` and `fraqInt`.[^ibkrwebapi] IBKR separately documents fractional shorts by fractional share quantity.[^ibkrfractionalshort] This establishes Web API cash-quantity stock buys and strongly indicates fractional share-quantity sells, but IBKR provides no explicit Web API fractional-short example. Separate authenticated paper previews of a reducing fractional sell and an opening fractional short are required before either is a local execution assumption.

That route has two load-bearing drawbacks:

1. **Retail operations:** an individual must run the Client Portal Gateway, authenticate through a browser and 2FA at least daily, and keep the session alive. One username cannot simultaneously own a TWS/IB Gateway and Web API brokerage session; a second username can separate them, but permissions and market-data subscriptions are user-specific.[^ibkrwebauth]
2. **Economics:** IBKR charges a US fractional stock trade the greater of 1% of trade value or USD 0.01. The percentage dominates almost every useful order, making a fractional round trip approximately 2% before spread, market impact and borrow.[^ibkrcommission] IBKR also warns that a non-marketable fractional limit component may wait until it becomes marketable, so fractional execution is a poor substitute for the passive liquidity provision on which short-horizon reversal relies.[^ibkrfractionaldisclosure]

For a USD 5,000 gross book, opening and later closing a fully refreshed fractional portfolio would consume about USD 100, or 2% of gross capital, before the other costs. Even 50% one-way turnover consumes roughly 0.5% of book value in fractional commissions. That is incompatible with a signal whose cited net edge is measured in tens of basis points per week. Whole-share tiered orders, by contrast, have a USD 0.35 minimum and a 1% cap; they retain a granularity problem but can be materially cheaper for positions above USD 35.[^ibkrcommission]

The immediate conclusion is not “build a Web API adapter.” The research candidate must first survive an executable whole-share universe and turnover model. Web API fractions may later be useful for slow, high-conviction residual cleanup, but they should not be used to manufacture apparent breadth in a fast reversal backtest.

Breadth should be separated into three layers:

1. **Information breadth:** rank hundreds or thousands of liquid stocks and estimate industry/factor-relative residuals.
2. **Ideal portfolio breadth:** form the diversified, neutral target that best represents the signal before account constraints.
3. **Executed breadth:** choose positions whose expected contribution survives commission, spread, borrow, sizing granularity and risk constraints while approximating the broad ideal target. At the present account size, whole shares are the economic default; fractional orders are a costly exception, not a free relaxation.

This is supported by both primary and practitioner work. Gârleanu and Pedersen show that costly trading should move partially toward an aim portfolio that incorporates signal decay rather than repeatedly jumping to a frictionless target.[^garleanu] Novy-Marx and Velikov find that a buy/hold spread, lower-cost-stock selection and slower partial rebalancing preserve more anomaly return than naive periodic reconstruction; the buy/hold spread is usually the most effective single technique.[^taxonomy] Rob Carver's integer minimum-tracking-error construction is directly relevant to the economically preferred whole-share implementation.[^carver]

The repository already owns instrument- and sleeve-level drift bands. Do not create another generic no-trade subsystem. The incremental strategy logic is:

- **membership hysteresis:** a stock needs an extreme score to enter but is retained until it crosses a much looser exit rank;
- **cost-aware replacement:** a challenger replaces an incumbent only if expected improvement over the expected holding horizon clears commission, half-spread, borrow and FX with a conservative multiplier;
- **cost-aware executable projection:** preserve as much of the neutral ideal portfolio as possible while omitting changes whose expected benefit does not clear sizing granularity, commission, spread and borrow; use whole shares by default and fractions only when their roughly 1%-per-side charge is explicitly justified;
- **net targets before orders:** let the commingled book resolve offsetting desired exposures before execution;
- **patient execution:** use limits where the signal horizon permits liquidity provision, with non-fills represented honestly.

## Standalone challenger: enhanced residual reversal

The current evidence does **not** support raw winner-minus-loser reversal or a six-stock implementation. Classic short-term reversal has weakened to the point of disappearing in most regions; its enhanced version improves when it stops trading against industry and factor momentum.[^reversing] The more useful liquidity study constructs a five-day industry-relative signal, removes returns around earnings announcements, and finds that volatility controls reversal speed while turnover controls persistence. Low-turnover stocks can continue reverting for almost three months, creating room to trade more patiently.[^liquidityreversal]

For this book, the primary standalone test should nevertheless be **monthly**, not weekly. Even cost-aware weekly reversal retains approximately `225-337%` weekly turnover in the published large-cap tests.[^degroot] The monthly residual benchmark is slower and supplies an honest 40-position reference. The five-day announcement-adjusted signal should first be tested as an entry/execution overlay, where it may improve already intended trades without demanding its own fast portfolio.

The preliminary pre-registrable shape is:

1. Build a survivorship-safe liquid US equity universe from information known on each date.
2. Estimate the latest one-month return residual relative to an industry and broad-factor model, standardized by residual volatility.
3. As a registered variant, exclude earnings-announcement and identified corporate-event returns so the strategy does not trade against information.
4. Remove or penalize trades that oppose short-term industry/factor momentum.
5. Rank the broad universe, enter only extreme residual losers and winners, and retain incumbents through a substantially wider exit rank.
6. Construct a dollar-, beta- and industry-controlled ideal long/short portfolio.
7. Convert it to a cost-aware executable portfolio by minimizing tracking error plus expected trading and borrow cost. The principal live control is whole-share sizing; any fractional order must clear its separate 1%-per-side cost and execution model.
8. Apply rank membership hysteresis first and the existing drift bands second.

The payer is temporary liquidity demand. The anchor is the stock's industry/factor-implied residual price, not an arbitrary pair ratio. The principal failure is a common liquidity or quant deleveraging event in which residuals diverge together, as in August 2007. That failure is economically complementary to persistent trend, but the pairing must be measured rather than assumed.

Four results make the candidate a useful challenger but not the first build:

- de Groot, Huij and Zhou report `30-50 bp/week` net reversal profits after restricting to large caps and using an exit rule that more than halves turnover.[^degroot]
- Novy-Marx and Velikov's full anomaly cost taxonomy is much harsher: raw short-run reversal is negative net of their cost model, while low-volatility industry-relative reversal and a combined high-frequency signal survive cost mitigation better.[^taxonomy]
- Frazzini, Israel and Moskowitz use nearly one trillion dollars of live institutional trades and find cost-aware construction raises net returns and capacity, but short-term reversal remains the most cost-constrained style.[^frazzini]
- The smallest directly supported residual-reversal reference still holds approximately 40 stocks; a 12-name version must prove that sparse whole-share projection preserves signal and neutrality.[^residualreversal]

The design must therefore beat a raw-reversal control and an execution-only use of the same signal. If a standalone funded book does not add more utility than using reversal merely to delay existing trades, retain it as execution logic and leave Demeter empty.

### Open feasibility questions being researched now

- Can the data layer construct a dead-stock-inclusive point-in-time universe without a prohibitively expensive license?
- What fraction of candidate names remain general collateral through time, and how much does name-specific borrow reduce the usable short universe?
- What low-price, liquid, general-collateral universe and executed `k` minimize whole-book tracking error after whole-share granularity, commissions, spread, borrow and missed-fill costs?
- Does that whole-share implementation retain enough information breadth, or does the small book force concentration back into specification risk?
- Does the expected residual half-life leave enough gross edge after observed spreads, commissions, borrow and missed limit orders?
- Does beta/industry neutralization preserve the premium or remove it?
- Does the stream add paired utility beside Atalanta, or merely duplicate its whipsaw losses?

## Why credit is no longer the lead

The proposed OAS-richness plus six-month momentum design is materially different from distribution-yield ranking. It uses a real market price of credit risk and asks for directional confirmation before adding HY exposure. That makes it a valid hypothesis, not a repair of the old yield metric.

It still does not deserve the lead. Acadian applied 2,500 spread entry/exit rules across five lookbacks and eight credit indices, producing 100,000 specifications; roughly `70%` underperformed buy-and-hold, the positive regions were fragile, and the apparently consistent family failed its longer out-of-sample extension.[^acadian] Newfound finds a narrower tactical-credit momentum region, but removing 2000-2003 and 2008-2009 reduces gross return from `2.9%` to `1.5%`, before strategy fees and account-level switching costs.[^newfound] That is too thin to justify another open-ended redesign after the local bucket-selection edge measured only about `3 bp/year`.

The bond-level factor literature does not rescue the wrapper trade. The credible studies rank many individual bonds using OAS, expected default or quality, credit excess momentum, liquidity and transaction costs. A recent replication finds that many published corporate-bond factors weaken materially.[^israel][^lancaster] Selecting between two duration/currency-matched funds is aggregate credit-premium timing, not bond-level systematic credit.

> [!warning] Point-in-time data is part of the hypothesis
> ICE OAS series should use ALFRED vintages or an immutable release archive. The Federal Reserve excess-bond-premium history revises as underlying information changes; downloading today's complete CSV and treating it as the historical live series would leak revisions.[^alfred][^ebp]

If a final cheap credit test is run, freeze one specification only: expanding point-in-time HY-minus-IG OAS percentile, six-month HY-minus-IG total-return confirmation, monthly decisions, duration/currency-matched wrappers, and the existing allocator volatility cap. Kill it if it cannot beat both static IG and static HY/IG controls after touch costs, if attribution is only higher average credit beta, or if a one-step horizon perturbation reverses the result. Passing would establish a tactical credit allocation, not a new generic convergence alpha.

## Research path 1: direct fixed-cash merger arbitrage

Merger arbitrage has the cleanest anchor. After a definitive offer, the target trades below the consideration because investors can transfer deal-failure, financing, delay and regulatory risk to specialized capital. Mitchell and Pulvino estimate about `4%` annual excess return after transaction costs and nonlinear downside adjustment, while showing that market beta rises sharply in severe declines.[^mitchell] The strategy is convergent, but it is still short a broken-deal option.

The first prototype should trade the target shares directly. Restricting the universe to fixed-cash consideration removes the acquirer hedge and makes a sparse whole-share implementation mechanically possible. It does not remove broken-deal risk. The executable design must:

- discover definitive deals from timestamped filings rather than rumors or pre-event speculation;
- exclude stock, mixed and structurally complex consideration in the first version;
- size by conservative break loss rather than recent volatility or success spread;
- return a fully invested sleeve-internal composition whenever at least one deal is eligible, because the whole-book allocator—not Demeter—owns the sleeve's capital budget;
- cap relative concentration at `1.5 / N` of the sleeve and redistribute clipped weight across the remaining selected deals rather than turning a `6/8/10/12` frontier into `40/20/0/0%` accidental cash;
- monitor amendments, votes, regulatory conditions, litigation, financing and closing or termination filings;
- charge executable entry ask, exit bid, commissions, FX and cash opportunity cost;
- reject the design if roughly ten independent qualifying deals are not normally expressible.

The initial ranker should be conservative expected value, not annualized spread. Start from a naïve equal-break-risk basket, then add only transparent deal-quality variables whose live values can be reconstructed from filings. For selected deal $i$ with conservative break loss $L_i$, solve $w_i = \min(c, \lambda / L_i)$, where $c = \min(1, 1.5/N)$ and $\lambda$ makes the selected weights sum to one. The unconstrained names retain equal break-loss contribution; capped weight is redistributed rather than left as cash. The allocator subsequently multiplies this internal composition by Demeter's whole-book sleeve weight. Cash is therefore evidence of no eligible opportunity or unavoidable whole-share execution residue, not a hidden second allocation decision. A completion model is useful only when it estimates probability independently of the market spread and survives time-split calibration. The market-implied probability is a diagnostic, not an alpha signal.

The direct research sequence is:

1. Reconstruct the point-in-time EDGAR event tape, including failed and amended deals.
2. Run the all-eligible, equal-break-risk cash-deal benchmark with whole shares, exact quotes and no leverage.
3. Compare `6/8/10/12`-deal caps with the full eligible set and report concentration loss.
4. Add clean-term exclusions, conservative close scenarios and transparent completion base rates.
5. Test the residual-entry overlay without changing which events qualify.
6. Evaluate the net event book beside locked Atalanta, including common crash months and individual breaks.

Merger-arbitrage wrappers remain comparators rather than the first implementation. They prove professional breadth but expose the cost problem: current products report dozens of positions, while retail-class operating and transaction costs can consume a modest gross premium.[^carmignac][^candriam] If the direct event tape cannot be made causal or the sparse book is dominated by a few breaks, do not rescue it by choosing a wrapper after seeing its return history.

## Research path 2: investment-trust discount convergence with a hard catalyst

This is the strongest route to developing an original alpha because the anchor and the data can be stated without pretending price alone must mean-revert.

The target universe is liquid conventional-equity UK investment trusts. Exclude VCTs, private-asset and property vehicles with stale appraisals, highly geared trusts and names whose spreads make a small position uneconomic. A candidate must be both unusually discounted relative to its own and sector history **and** have a public, timestamped route to realization:

- a tender or exit offer near NAV;
- a scheduled continuation vote;
- a merger, wind-down or liquidation proposal;
- a material and persistent buyback program;
- a filed discount-control mechanism with measurable terms.

Practitioners support the distinction. AIC and Deutsche Numis identify exit offers, continuation votes and tenders as corporate catalysts, while current practitioner discussion warns that occasional small buybacks and nominal discount policies do not force rerating.[^aiccatalyst][^hawksmoor] A wide discount alone can simply price fees, gearing, illiquidity, governance or an unreliable NAV.

The first deliverable must be a **data feasibility proof**:

1. Download AIC monthly information-release archives and confirm publication dates, identifiers, NAV, shares outstanding and dead/merged-trust coverage.[^aicmir]
2. Build a timestamped RNS/company-announcement ledger for the catalyst, including withdrawals and failed votes.
3. Confirm that at least three to five liquid eligible trusts were normally available without current-universe survivorship.
4. Measure actual bid/ask, GBP conversion, broker commissions and the `0.5%` stamp duty paid on purchases of UK company shares.[^stamp][^trustcost]
5. Decompose returns into underlying NAV return and discount change. Kill the strategy if the apparent alpha is ordinary equity/sector beta.

Only after those five checks should a strategy be specified. A plausible first frozen rule would use lagged monthly AIC data, a five-year robust own-history discount score, a same-sector relative score, and a pre-existing hard catalyst, with quarterly-or-slower turnover. The event creates the convergence claim; the z-score only sets the entry price.

## What the practitioner and arXiv searches added

Practitioner work was most valuable where academic studies abstract from implementation:

- credit managers show that index spread timing is unstable across rules and markets;[^acadian]
- merger-arbitrage fund documents reveal how retail fees consume a modest gross spread;[^carmignac][^candriam]
- investment-trust specialists distinguish material tenders and persistent buybacks from cosmetic discount policy, and document spreads from about `0.3%` in a liquid trust to many percentage points in thin names.[^trustcost]

Recent arXiv work is useful mainly as a boundary. An attention-factor statistical-arbitrage preprint reports strong after-cost results, but it learns and trades a broad stock universe with turnover-aware long/short construction; it supports the need for breadth rather than a two-pair implementation.[^attention] A 2025 put-writing preprint reports attractive short-dated results, but it is new, unreplicated and depends on options, sizing and execution that the current account cannot reproduce.[^putpreprint] Both are hypothesis generators, not promotion evidence.

The stronger counterweight for option selling is the Chicago Fed's 1987-2022 traded-option study. It finds a break around 2010 and delta-hedged put and straddle alpha approximately zero in the later period, consistent with improved dealer hedging and lower frictions.[^vrpdecline] This does not prove a fully collateralized put-write wrapper has zero expected return: that wrapper also owns cash and equity beta. It does kill the assumption that a recent smooth wrapper record demonstrates a large independent variance alpha.

## Local evidence closes three familiar loops

1. **UEQC:** the fixed `15%` and `25%` commodity-carry breadth path was extended through 2026-07-01 and killed. The high-weight point estimate was attractive, but it remained statistically inseparable from the control and the response was non-monotone. Re-entry requires a new observable curve-regime hypothesis, not a renamed metric.
2. **E50PW:** the fixed put-write weight sweep was killed after extension. The `20%` neighbor lost while `30%` produced an isolated jump; mean downside L-skew turned positive and two folds changed sign. A volatility-managed rule would be a new hypothesis, but current external evidence is insufficient to prioritize it.
3. **Credit bucket selection:** the active richness advantage was approximately `3 bp/year` before its additional fixed fees. More buckets or a no-trade band cannot make that signal economically material.

These results remain valid under the renamed convergent role. Removing a negative-skew gate changes the job specification; it does not convert failed net utility into evidence.

## Ranked decision ladder

### Now

Keep Demeter empty or in cash. Preserve Atalanta and the rest of the roster. An empty job is better than a return stream that only resembles the desired payoff in labels.

### First research build

Build the point-in-time fixed-cash merger event tape from EDGAR, including amendments, completions and failures. Run an equal-break-risk, no-leverage, whole-share benchmark before estimating deal probability. Then test the `6/8/10/12`-position frontier and the residual-entry overlay. Do not begin with a flexible completion model.

### Parallel stock challenger

Prove that the enhanced monthly residual-reversal universe, historical identifiers, event exclusions, industry mapping, commissions and borrow data can be represented without lookahead. Compare `12/20/30/40` whole-share portfolios with the continuous target, raw reversal, an execution-only overlay and cash. Kill the standalone version if sparse transfer or neutrality collapses.

### Secondary proprietary branch

Test whether AIC plus RNS can produce a survivorship-safe, point-in-time investment-trust catalyst ledger and whether three to five liquid positions are normally available after real trading costs. This remains a genuine slow-convergence path, but it follows the broader and more systematic equity feasibility proof.

### Cheap optional falsification

Run one frozen OAS-richness plus momentum credit test only if the required ALFRED vintage data and duration/currency matching are already available. Treat failure as closure of aggregate credit timing, not an invitation to sweep more horizons.

### Future unlock

When the book and permissions can absorb a diversified long/short alternative-risk-premia fund, validate a low-minimum share class. That is closer to the winning research substrate than any sparse DIY replica, although its overlap with Atalanta and expansion must be measured.

## Promotion and stop rules

A candidate can enter the roster only if it satisfies all of the following without tuning the rule to the same history:

- positive held-out certainty-equivalent return after actual wrapper fees, touch costs, FX and taxes;
- positive marginal whole-book utility beside locked Atalanta, with block-bootstrap uncertainty reported;
- return attribution that identifies the promised premium rather than generic equity, duration, FX or credit beta;
- an observable loss trigger and a survivable historical or scenario loss budget;
- a causal live data path with immutable point-in-time inputs;
- robustness to one-step parameter perturbations and removal of the single best episode.

If the direct merger tape or sparse event book fails, the standalone residual challenger fails, and the investment-trust data proof fails, stop the Demeter search. Use cash, keep reversal as execution information, and revisit only when capital, permissions or a genuinely breadth-preserving wrapper changes the feasible set.
