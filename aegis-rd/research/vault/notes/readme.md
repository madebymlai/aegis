# Notes - research scratchpad

A holding pen for knowledge that is worth keeping but has not earned a full
`research/` article yet. A note here is article-shaped (prose, footnoted sources,
`[[wikilinks]]`) but explicitly **provisional**: a finding from a search sweep, a
mechanism we reasoned out, a lever we have not yet run. The bar is "do not lose this",
not "we have verified this".

## How a note differs from an article

- **Article** (`research/`): synthesized, defended, distilled into hypotheses that are
  tested as runs. The knowledge flows one way and lands here when it is settled.
- **Note** (`notes/`): a scratchpad capture. May be a single session's finding, may
  contain an unrun hypothesis, may later graduate into an article or die in the
  [[graveyard|graveyard]]. Always carries a `status` line so its maturity is legible.

## Conventions

Same as the vault: kebab-case filenames, no em dashes, Obsidian-flavored markdown,
footnoted sources. Add a `> [!note] Status` callout at the top saying whether the
finding is reasoned-only, partially tested, or awaiting a run. When a note's central
hypothesis is run, either promote the durable parts into a `research/` article or move
the note's verdict into the run diary and link it.
