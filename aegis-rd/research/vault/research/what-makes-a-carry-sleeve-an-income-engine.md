# What Makes a Carry Sleeve an Income Engine

> [!abstract] One-line takeaway
> The income-engine shape a carry sleeve is hired for - steady premium in calm, a *budgeted* loss in the tail - is destroyed by the objective used to select it: the Sharpe-maximizing payoff is, by mathematical construction, "regular modest profits punctuated by occasional crashes", so Sharpe-ranking a short-gamma config sweep selects the candidate that best hides its tail. Rank the pole on a crash-averse utility (the manipulation-proof MPPM), gate it on a multi-month tail budget, and source the signal from spread *richness* (the value half of carry), because the raw spread *level* is mostly repriced credit beta.

The trend article ([[what-makes-a-trend-sleeve-convex]]) ended in a selection rule: a convex sleeve ranked on Sharpe silently becomes the least convex book, so it must be ranked on its hired shape. This article is the mirror for the concave pole, and the trap here is worse. For trend, Sharpe-ranking merely *neglects* the hired shape; for carry, Sharpe-ranking *is the attack surface*. A short-gamma sleeve does not drift away from its job under a Sharpe objective - it over-performs its job, hiding ever more tail to buy ever more smoothness, and the optimizer rewards every step. Our demeter pole ranks its sweeps on `sharpe_ratio` today; the roster's income pole ([[the-tiered-strategy-roster]], [[carry-as-the-short-gamma-income-pole]]) is selected by exactly the objective the literature says it can game.

## Sharpe is the adversary's objective, not the evaluator's

The result is old and exact. Goetzmann, Ingersoll, Spiegel and Welch derive the payoff that maximizes the Sharpe ratio when returns can be shaped freely (with options or dynamic trading): it has a truncated right tail and a fat left tail, and they state the mechanism plainly - "expected returns being held constant, high Sharpe ratio strategies are, by definition, strategies that generate regular modest profits punctuated by occasional crashes".[^gisw] That is not a description of a pathological corner case; it is a description of a premium-collection sleeve. Spurgin had already shown the trade empirically: an out-of-the-money put-writing overlay smooths measured Sharpe upward with a two-in-three chance of surviving three years before the tail ever prints.[^spurgin] Leland closes the theory from the pricing side - mean-variance measures systematically *reward* short-gamma payoffs and penalize long-gamma ones even when true risk-adjusted alpha is zero.[^leland]

The reason this bites us specifically is that a parameter sweep is an optimization over payoff shapes. Eling and Schuhmacher's well-known counter-result - that across 2,763 live hedge funds, Sharpe and twelve alternative measures produce virtually identical rankings (98 of the Sharpe top-100 are also in the Omega top-100) - is real, and it is the best opposing source to this article's whole argument.[^eling] But its universe is a *cross-section of funds nobody optimized against the measure*; the authors attribute the result to hedge-fund returns being approximately elliptical, in which case the first two moments carry the ranking. A config sweep whose objective is Sharpe is the adversarial case their finding does not cover: the optimizer's job is precisely to find the non-elliptical candidate the measure cannot see. On our own book the mechanism has already been observed once - the skew-neutral floor blend over-bought carry because "UPI/Sharpe are calm-drawdown-path-dominated and over-reward carry's calm smoothing" ([[the-tiered-strategy-roster]], blend-rule revision).

Two further failures stack on top for this sleeve in particular. Lo shows that the Sharpe ratio's own sampling machinery breaks under the serial correlation typical of premium-collection streams - naive annualization can overstate a fund's annual Sharpe by up to 65%, enough to reshuffle rankings on its own.[^lo] And Bailey and Lopez de Prado show that selecting the best of N swept configurations inflates the expected maximum Sharpe mechanically, with fat-tailed and negatively-skewed winners inflating most - their Deflated Sharpe Ratio exists to correct exactly the "pick the sweep's Sharpe-winner" procedure we run.[^dsr]

## The manipulation-proof shape

The same Goetzmann et al. paper that names the disease supplies the cure. A performance measure is manipulation-proof when no payoff-shaping can raise the score without adding genuine expected utility; they prove the measure is essentially unique, and it is a power-utility certainty equivalent computed on the realized return stream:[^gisw]

$$\hat{\Theta} = \frac{1}{(1-\rho)\,\Delta t}\,\ln\!\left(\frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=1}^{T}\left(\frac{1+r_t}{1+r_{f,t}}\right)^{1-\rho}\right)$$

$\hat{\Theta}$ reads as the annualized risk-free-excess growth rate a CRRA evaluator with relative risk aversion $\rho$ would accept as certain in exchange for the stream - income net of the tail it hides, in one number. It needs nothing beyond the net return series, it has no threshold or horizon knob, and its single parameter is interpretable (they suggest evaluators in the $\rho \approx 2$-$4$ range). A day approaching total loss drives the score to $-\infty$: the measure prices the left tail the way the sleeve's owner actually experiences it, rather than averaging it away. This is the concave pole's analog of the trend pole's `trend_convexity_payoff` - a benchmark-free ranker that scores the *hired shape* - and it satisfies the same stabilizer discipline (a smooth utility-weighted mean, no cutoff to choose, low estimator variance) that made the crisis-payoff metric split-stable.

Why not the more familiar alternatives? The moment-corrected Sharpe family (the Pezier-White adjusted Sharpe, $ASR = SR[1 + \tfrac{S}{6}SR - \tfrac{K-3}{24}SR^2]$) looks like the cheap fix, but Smetters and Zhang show Taylor-expansion corrections of this kind can misrank, destabilize, and even diverge outside near-normal territory - fine as a screen, not as the arbiter.[^smetters] The tail-ratio family (Omega, CVaR-based STARR and Rachev ratios) aims at the right economics but ranks on tail quantiles a daily sample estimates poorly: Varga-Haszonits and Kondor prove estimation error under downside measures diverges at a critical parameters-to-sample ratio, exactly the small-crisis-count regime a 2-year split lives in.[^kondor] The division of labor that follows the vault's existing pattern: **rank** on the smooth, low-variance utility ($\hat{\Theta}$), **gate** on the tail budget measured where a short-gamma blowup actually plays out - the left decile of overlapping multi-month compounded windows, band-averaged over the same 2-6 month horizons the trend metrics use so no single window is load-bearing - and **trust-check** the sweep winner post hoc with the Deflated Sharpe Ratio,[^dsr] the concave mirror of ranking trend on the point estimate and reporting the block-bootstrap CI.

## Strong carry is richness plus quality, not spread level

The metric fixes selection; the signal itself has a parallel defect, and the credit literature has just relitigated it. The raw credit premium is modest: duration-hedged US investment-grade credit earned about 137bp per year over 1936-2014 (Sharpe ~0.37) and high yield about 248bp over 1988-2014 (Sharpe ~0.26) - real (the excess is not spanned by the term and equity premia; the intercept survives at ~7bp/month) but the weakest of the classic premia on a raw basis.[^ar] And the cross-sectional "carry" version of the signal - buy the highest spread - is mostly beta: AQR's own decomposition reports corporate-bond carry with a 0.90 correlation to the aggregate credit market and five times the volatility of value for comparable return.[^aqrstyle] Quoniam's independent replication is blunter still in high yield, where OAS-based carry and value factors print *negative* alphas - the heterogeneous HY universe prices spread level as compensation, not mispricing.[^henke]

The sharpest recent evidence goes one layer deeper. Dickerson, Robotti and Rossetti build a 108-factor corporate-bond zoo and show that after correcting two systematic biases (the same noisy transaction price entering both the sorting signal and the return denominator, and look-ahead in ex-post return filtering), *most published corporate bond factors are spanned by the plain bond-market factor* - and the survivors are primarily **credit-spread-based value signals**, not spread-level carry.[^drr] The constructive statement of the same result is Israel, Palhares and Richardson: a long-only portfolio tilting the same universe by carry *plus* defensive (low leverage, low duration, high profitability) *plus* momentum *plus* value earned 5.26% net of costs at Sharpe 1.03 versus the cap-weighted benchmark's 0.69 - with defensive, momentum and value showing stronger return associations than carry alone.[^ipr]

For a sleeve like ours the translation is direct. The defensible half of the signal is the one `demeter_eu.credit_carry` already computes: spread **richness relative to its own history** - a value signal in the Dickerson-Robotti-Rossetti sense - not the spread level. Anything that leans on raw level is leaning on the 0.90-correlated beta half.[^aqrstyle] And the cheapest untested lever is a quality tilt: the factor evidence says a defensive leg (shorter duration, higher quality inside HY) adds more than a second helping of carry does.[^ipr][^henke]

## Timing pays in the tail; sizing lags it

The conditioning evidence, which is FX-based for want of a credit literature, splits cleanly along a line worth respecting. *Regime avoidance* has real support: excluding carry positions during high-FX-volatility regimes (alone, or jointly with extreme rate differentials) beats the unconditional carry trade on mean, standard deviation, skewness, kurtosis, Sharpe and holding-period return in virtually every setting Mulder and Tims test, 25 countries, 1985-2015.[^mt] The mechanism is the one Christiansen, Ranaldo and Soderlind measure with a smooth-transition regression: carry's equity beta is regime-dependent and *rises sharply in the volatile regime* - the strategy is most exposed exactly when exposure is most expensive.[^crs] Against this stands Asness's factor-timing caution: contrarian, valuation-based timing of the factor itself is "deceptively difficult", and its backtested gains mostly vanish once implemented as a tradeable strategy.[^asness] The reconciliation is that the two claims time different things - avoiding a measurably hostile regime for a crash-prone trade is not the same act as tactically fading a factor's valuation - but a carry sleeve should treat conditioning as an unproven transfer to credit until tested; no credit-specific equivalent of Mulder-Tims surfaced in this sweep.

Sizing deserves the same honesty. Realized-vol scaling (our `vol_target` exposure stage) raises average risk-adjusted return across factors, carry included,[^mt] but a trailing-vol estimator is coincident-to-lagging with respect to the regime that matters: the beta spike Christiansen et al. measure arrives with the volatility, so the scalar de-risks *after* the first leg of a gap move, never before it.[^crs] The sleeve should keep the scaling for what it does (drawdown-path smoothing, average Sharpe) and stop expecting it to do what it cannot (protect the first crash day). Fast-tail protection is the trend pole's and tail sleeve's job - the roster's division of labor, restated from the concave side.

## Limitations

- The best opposing source is genuinely good: Eling-Schuhmacher's rank-correlation result says measure choice rarely matters on live fund cross-sections.[^eling] This article's rebuttal - that a Sharpe-objective sweep is the adversarial case their elliptical universe excludes - is an argument from mechanism, not a measured demonstration on our book. The metric A/B hypothesis below is the measurement.
- MPPM's $\rho$ is a knob. Rankings are reported to be fairly stable across $\rho \in [2,5]$,[^gisw] but that stability should be verified per sweep (a cheap sensitivity sweep, not a new estimator).
- The Dickerson-Robotti-Rossetti factor-zoo result is a 2026 working paper, not yet peer-reviewed; its bias framework is compelling and open-sourced, but the value-survives/carry-does-not split should be held provisionally.[^drr]
- Several load-bearing construction sources carry aligned COI (AQR papers marketing multi-factor credit, Quoniam running credit-factor funds); the raw-premium figures and the manipulation-proof theory are the COI-free backbone.[^gisw][^ar]
- McLean-Pontiff's post-publication decay (~58% average) applies to every headline Sharpe above; the IPR 1.03 should be read as an upper bound, not an expectation.[^mp]
- FX carry conditioning may not transfer to credit; the timing hypothesis below is an extension of the literature, not a replication.

## Strategy hypotheses this could seed

- [x] **The ranker A/B.** On the identical duration-stripped credit book (`carry_income.yaml` vs the `sharpe_ratio`-locked `carry.yaml` champion), the MPPM-ranked ($\rho=3$) winner differs from the Sharpe-ranked winner and dominates it on the tail axis. Pre-register: held-out `carry_income_utility` at or above the Sharpe-champion's, held-out multi-month tail budget (`carry_tail_budget`, 2-6mo left-decile mean) strictly shallower, at held-out Sharpe within 0.10 of the lock. If the two rankers select the *same* cell, that is evidence for Eling-Schuhmacher on our book and the metric becomes a free trust check rather than a new objective. **Tested 2026-07-03, verdict iterate** ([[runs/demeter/2026-07-03|diary]]): the champions differ and the MPPM pick is genuinely shallower-tailed (tail budget -9.9% vs -12.4%/yr, crisis-split maxDD 14.2% vs 21.7%) inside the 0.10 Sharpe band - but it FAILS its own held-out utility bar (+0.0081 < +0.0100): on a gross-capped, long-only, vol-targeted book there is no leverage/optionality axis to hide tail in, so MPPM spends its ranking on the noisy *scale* axis (vol_target) that scale-invariant Sharpe ignores. The metrics are kept as promotion gates; the ranker swap is not promoted. Next lever: a shape-only MPPM (vol-standardized stream) that ranks payoff shape and leaves scale to the mandate. **Sequel, same day** ([[runs/floor/2026-07-03]], [[the-skew-is-the-product]]): the lesson goes one level deeper than ranker choice. On the shape-only grid where MPPM *was* the legitimate ranker, its champion still defected from the pole's portfolio role (skew shaved, corr-to-trend flipped positive) - because manipulation-proofness protects against payoff-shaping *within* the stream, not against selecting a stream whose diversification value is gone. No standalone metric, MPPM included, can see the role. Promotion needs composite-level gates on top of the standalone ones: hard qskew floor, corr-to-trend ceiling measured in the composite's stress states.
- [ ] **The tail budget gates the family.** Add `carry_tail_budget` to the carry/mean-reversion family gate in [[verifying-strategy-family-membership]]: a concave pole must show its left tail (it is being paid for one), but the annualized 2-6mo band left-decile mean must clear a floor set from the book's survivable loss - the "budgeted, not open-ended" requirement of [[carry-as-the-short-gamma-income-pole]] stated as a number. **Extended 2026-07-03**: the gate set is incomplete without the composite side - the same pole passed every standalone gate while losing its role ([[the-skew-is-the-product]]); the family gate must pair the tail-budget floor with a hard qskew <= -0.5 and a corr-to-trend ceiling measured in stress states.
- [ ] **DSR the sweep winner.** Compute the Deflated Sharpe Ratio of each sweep's chosen cell (trials = grid size, cross-sectional Sharpe variance from the sweep itself); a winner whose DSR is indistinguishable from best-of-N luck is not promotable regardless of ranker.[^dsr]
- [ ] **Quality beats more carry.** Adding a defensive leg (short-duration HY or quality-screened HY UCITS ETF, instrument to be verified investable) to the IHYU+LQDH book lifts income-per-tail (`carry_income_utility` up at flat-or-shallower `carry_tail_budget`) more than raising `carry_gain` does - the IPR/Henke defensive-dominates-carry claim on our universe.[^ipr][^henke]
- [ ] **Richness is the live half of the signal.** A sweep that leans exposure on spread richness (current construction) beats an otherwise-identical sweep leaning on raw spread level, on held-out utility - the Dickerson-Robotti-Rossetti value-vs-carry split reproduced at the timing margin.[^drr]
- [ ] **Vol-regime avoidance transfers to credit (or does not).** A high-vol mute on the credit book (exposure to cash when trailing book vol crosses a percentile gate) improves held-out skew and utility as it does for FX carry;[^mt] pre-register the Asness-side null - the gate's gains vanish net of the calm-period income it forfeits.[^asness] **Superseded before testing, 2026-07-03** ([[the-ucits-constrained-carry-sleeve]]): the construction sweep found convergent evidence that de-risking into declines is a skew-*raising* operation (risk-managed momentum cuts the -97% crash to -45% precisely by truncating the left tail) - a mute would flip the concave pole toward the convex shape the trend sleeve already owns, dissolving the floor's skew budget. Any timing mute belongs in a separate convex-budgeted sleeve, not inside this pole; do not spend a run here except as the pre-registered negative control in the construction article.
