# Persistent-Crisis Responder - porting brief

> [!abstract] Thesis

> response-function design - not breadth or substrate - and is paid by structurally
> price-insensitive counterparties. Its whipsaw losses are the **premium** for crisis convexity,
> not a fixable defect.
