# 2026-06-10

## Goal

First run of the new aegis family: a defensive, bear-market sleeve on the usual ten-ETF
universe. Tests hypothesis 1 from [[crash-resistance-in-our-etf-universe]]: long/short
time-series momentum across all ten ETFs (inverse-vol sized, signed weights) produces
positive returns in the sustained 2022 bear and the 2020 continuation, with the known
structural hole in fast V-shaped shocks (Q4 2018). The bear episodes in our 2018-2025
window: Q4 2018 (fast, ~-20%), 2020-Q1 COVID (fast, -34%), Jan-Oct 2022 (slow grind,
~-25%, stocks and bonds down together) - aegis is built around the 2022-style grind.

## Runs

### 20260610T174939648182Z_etf_aegis_v1_tsmom

- **Hypothesis**: Long/short TSMOM across SPY/IWM/EEM/TLT/GLD/DBC/VNQ/UUP/XLE/XLU,
  inverse-vol sized and normalized to gross 1.0, is a divergent crash sleeve: it
  rotates into trending havens and shorts falling risk legs without a hand-coded
  regime rule. `short_scale` spans 0.0/0.5/1.0 so the run itself says whether the
  short side earns its financing carry or the long-only fallback is enough.
- **Keep/kill criteria** (pre-registered, before launching): keep if the best
  candidate's Held-out Sharpe >= 0.5 with max_dd <= 25% and >= 5 trades per split;
  kill if Held-out Sharpe < 0 across best and median; otherwise iterate. Secondary
  read (not a gate): Held-out splits covering 2022 should be positive - if the
  sleeve loses in the one sustained bear it was built for, that is a kill signal
  regardless of full-sample Sharpe.
- **What changed since previous run**: first aegis run - new components
  `aegis.tsmom_score`, `aegis.realized_vol`, `aegis.tsmomCrisis`, new config
  `etf_aegis_v1_tsmom` (grid search, 675 combos, schema 8, direction both,
  gross_cap 1.0, short borrow 0.5%/yr).
- **Result**: 675/675 candidates researched, 0 degenerate, 0 invalid. Held-out
  Sharpe: best +0.931, median +0.528, worst -0.889 (best in-sample +1.241, gap
  +0.31). Best candidate params: lookback 252, vol window 10, entry_band 0.0,
  short_scale 0.0, tau 0.15 - the optimizer picked the **long-only fallback**;
  median did too (short_scale 0.0). Worst is the fast long/short book
  (lookback 42, short_scale 1.0, tau 0.05): -5.5% with 1136 trades - fast
  shorts bleed on whipsaw plus carry. Best candidate per held-out year:
  2019 +3.01, 2020 +0.73, 2021 +1.14, **2022 +0.09 (total return +0.40%,
  max_dd 7.3%)**, 2023 -0.31. Gates: held-out Sharpe >= 0.5 pass, max_dd <= 25%
  pass (worst split 8.1%), trades >= 5/split pass (>= 201). Kill condition not
  triggered: 2022 held-out is positive, barely.
- **Verdict**: keep, with an iterate flag. The sleeve *survives* the 2022 bear
  (+0.4% vs SPY ~-19%, single-digit drawdown) and passes every pre-registered
  gate, so the candidate is lock-worthy
  (`lock: 20260610T174939648182Z_etf_aegis_v1_tsmom`). But the divergent-signal
  ambition is not met: +0.09 Sharpe in 2022 is a cushion, not a clear positive
  swing, and the short side as implemented never earned its carry - the data
  chose long-only trend rotation into havens/cash over true long/short. The
  fast-crash hole is untested here (Q4 2018 only appears in a selection set,
  never held-out).

## Day summary

Built the aegis family from the two crisis-alpha articles and ran five
pre-registered sweeps in one session. What survived: **v4, the crisis blend**
(50/50 budget between a 252d long-only trend core across all ten ETFs and a
top-3 momentum rotation over the union haven basket TLT/GLD/UUP/DBC/XLE) -
held-out Sharpe +1.008, 2022 bear year +0.82 Sharpe / +8.2%, max_dd 5.7%, and
even its worst representative is positive. What died, each by a pre-registered
criterion: the short side (v1 + v3: never beats long-only, loses in 2022
itself, even at its best case) and the haven-leg trend gate (v5: never
selected). Key structural finding: in this thin ten-ETF book, defense is
expressed by *rotating long into havens and cash*, not by shorting - and the
union haven basket is what closes the 2022 hole (v2: +22.1% in 2022, vs the
growth-only basket as worst representative). The remaining weakness across the
whole family is 2023-style trendless chop, which is reversal whipsaw and needs
a different mechanism (vol-regime throttle or breadth gate), not a stronger
trend filter.
