# 2026-06-11

## Goal

Test the hypotheses seeded by the two new articles, one per campaign gap:
[[always-on-convexity-from-listed-instruments]] (the 2018 fast-crash hole -
generalize the UUP-floor insurance to listed convex instruments) and
[[relative-vs-absolute-momentum-in-small-universes]] (the 2023 chop hole -
relative selection inside the absolute gate). Component change: crisisBlend
2.4.0 adds a generalized hedge floor (`hedge_floor`, held in the one universe
symbol outside the ten base symbols, excluded from core/haven/breadth so the
floor-0 candidate reproduces the champion book) and top-k relative selection
inside the core's trend gate (`core_k`, 0 = off).

Data gate (verified via vbt YFData pull, 2026-06-11): VIXY 2011-01, VIXM
2011-01, BTAL 2011-09, EDV 2010-01 all cover the 2016-start extended window.
TAIL starts 2017-04 - with the 252-day warmup it cannot cover the 2017 or
2018 splits, so the TAIL-floor hypothesis is **not testable on our window**
(checkbox annotated as such, not killed).

All runs: extended window 2016-01-01 to 2025-01-01, held-out splits 0-6 =
calendar 2017-2023, champion pinned (core_wt 0.5, top_k 3, entry_band 0.0,
breadth_norm 0.4, tau 0.15, lookback 252, window 30), ranking sharpe_ratio.
Reference control (v13 floor-0 row): 2017 +0.81, 2018 -1.55, 2019 +3.45,
2020 +0.71, 2021 +1.07, 2022 +0.77, 2023 -0.51, composite 0.151. Gates are
judged against each run's own in-run control row.

## Day summary

The convexity article's instrument sweep is settled in one session, and the
answer is the incumbent: **no listed convex instrument beats the UUP floor
as 2018 insurance on our book**. VIXY is the only thing that moves the 2018
split and it costs the entire composite (the roll-decay premium is not
carryable); VIXM is cheap but inert at floor weights; BTAL fails the
insurance gate and is dominated head-to-head, though it logged a real
surprise (mild both-tails composite lift, no calm-year death - a
diversifier, not a hedge); EDV failed exactly as the trigger-axis regime
map predicted, which strengthens the article's central taxonomy. Four
graveyard rows. The 2018 hole remains priced-insurance-only territory:
champion bare, or champion + UUP floor 0.3.

The relative-momentum thread rose and fell inside one day, and the
pre-registration discipline is what caught it: core_k 3 passed every gate
in v26 (2023 -0.51 -> -0.14, 2018 -1.55 -> -1.14), but the spec-luck
floor (v28) killed the promotion - the 2023 gain exists only at the 252d
rank lookback (63d/126d make 2023 *worse* than the champion, dispersion
0.79 vs the +0.37 gain). This is the GEM fragility result reproduced on
our own book. Two findings survive the kill: the rank is informative
(h3's null is dead - per-split swings up to 1.49), and concentration
robustly damps the 2018 split across all rank specs (+0.35 to +0.41) at
broad-trend-year cost. v27's confound lesson stands: on a
haven-containing universe, cross-sectional rank carries real slow-bear
defense (2022 Sharpe survived gate removal; drawdown did not).

Operational: all nine aegis components (plus 26 dormant vanguard ones)
migrated to the new single-entry-point component contract (aegis-rd-5u4);
control rows reproduce yesterday's champion numbers exactly, so the
migration is behavior-clean. TAIL was ruled untestable at the data gate
(2017-04 start cannot cover the 2018 split).
