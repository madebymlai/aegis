# Survivorship-free market data for historical cash mergers

Date: 2026-07-20

## Decision

For the **recent 2024-2026 proof**, use the existing **Massive (formerly Polygon) Stocks Basic** access first. It is the cleanest zero-cost test because its current free plan includes all US tickers, reference data, corporate actions, end-of-day data, and a rolling two years of history. Its reference API can return inactive tickers and point-in-time ticker records, including CIK, Composite FIGI, Share Class FIGI, and the last-traded date; aggregates can be requested unadjusted. As of 2026-07-20, however, the rolling window reaches only about 2024-07-20, so it does **not** cover all of calendar 2024. ([pricing](https://massive.com/pricing?product=stocks), [all tickers](https://massive.com/docs/rest/stocks/tickers/all-tickers.md), [OHLC aggregates](https://massive.com/docs/rest/stocks/aggregates/custom-bars.md), [unadjusted flat files](https://massive.com/docs/flat-files/stocks/overview))

For a **longer personal research run**, the best low-friction first trial is **EODHD's $19.99/month EOD plan**. EODHD explicitly documents enumeration of delisted US symbols, continued EOD access after delisting, separate dividends and splits, US symbol-change history, and 30+ years on the paid EOD plan. Its delisted-company documentation also states an important coverage boundary: pre-2018 delistings have EOD only; fundamentals/actions are available for post-2018 delistings and intraday data for post-2021 delistings. ([delisted-company API](https://eodhd.com/financial-apis/delisted-stock-companies-data-2), [current pricing](https://eodhd.com/))

If identity quality and research defensibility justify more expense, validate **Sharadar SEP/ACTIONS/TICKERS** next. If a Windows-only local data service is acceptable, validate **Norgate Platinum**. **CRSP** is the benchmark, but CRSP itself says its databases target institutions and directs individual investors to other services. ([Sharadar](https://www.sharadar.com/data), [Norgate packages](https://norgatedata.com/subscribe/subscribe.php), [CRSP access](https://www.crsp.org/subscription-information/))

## What “survivorship-free” must mean here

The feed must retain a target after it disappears from today's market and must let the prototype distinguish reused symbols. For every event-derived candidate it needs:

- point-in-time identity and inactive/delisted discovery;
- raw daily OHLCV through the final quoted day;
- splits and cash dividends as separate events;
- a documented terminal outcome, or enough information to hand settlement to the EDGAR lifecycle.

No retail provider reviewed documents a CRSP-equivalent delisting return and final-payment record. Therefore the proposed boundary is:

1. market data supplies identity, unadjusted OHLCV, liquidity, and ordinary corporate actions;
2. EDGAR supplies merger terms, completion/termination, effective time, and cash consideration;
3. the backtest closes a successful cash deal at contractual consideration when the completion becomes causally known, rather than inventing a return from the last exchange print.

The last traded close is not necessarily the cash received, and an empty bar after delisting is not a zero return.

## Recommended validation before paying

Run one fixed census of recent known outcomes through Massive Basic first:

- successful cash acquisitions whose targets are now inactive;
- at least one failed deal that remained listed;
- one ticker change/reuse case;
- one target with a dividend or split during the event window.

For each candidate, verify: EDGAR CIK/target identity, inactive discoverability, the announcement-minus-252-trading-day lookback, every active-day raw OHLCV bar, action reconciliation, final quoted date, and contractual settlement. The prototype should record provider gaps explicitly rather than silently dropping a deal.

If Massive passes, it is sufficient for the recent proof. Then compare the identical census against EODHD and a Sharadar sample before selecting the long-history vendor. A provider is acceptable only if the event-derived target union—not merely today's ticker universe—reconciles with no unexplained omissions.
